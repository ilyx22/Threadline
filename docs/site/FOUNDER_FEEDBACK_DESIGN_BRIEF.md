# Threadline founder-feedback design brief

This brief is distilled from the complete Threadline ChatGPT project history: four conversations, 72 pages and 703 founder messages reviewed on 24 September 2026. It is the source of truth for the public-site direction.

## The correction

The founder has not asked for a conventional luxury consultancy site or a generic software dashboard. He repeatedly asks for a site that is unusually easy to follow, friendly, interactive and memorable. He likes the explanatory choreography of Birdhouse and the interactive clarity of Hydra. The recent criticism of childish graphics rejects the quality of execution, not the underlying desire for character and motion.

The correct balance is:

- 70% premium editorial and consultancy
- 20% interactive product visualisation
- 10% playful, tactile character

The tone is corporate and San Francisco startup-like, with the operational discipline of a serious professional-services firm. It must still feel authored and human.

## Visual rules

- Use physical cards, sorting, routing, stacking, stamping and return loops to explain cause and effect.
- Animation must show what the system does. Avoid floating decoration, scroll tricks and perpetual noise.
- Keep type inside graphics sober and highly legible. No novelty display type in diagrams, cramped labels or clipped lines.
- Use very few rounded containers. Prefer paper, hairlines, deep ink, warm neutral ground and controlled signal colours.
- The interface should reveal sophistication progressively. A cold visitor must understand it with motion disabled.
- A memorable visual moment is useful. A mascot-led or childish visual world is not.

## Protected homepage ideas

1. **Make the expertise that wins the work visible before the sales call.**
2. **Inside the firm → what the market sees.** The gap must be physically obvious.
3. **Market memory.** The goal is familiarity among a small pool of relevant buyers, not fame.
4. **Talk · record when useful · approve · sell.** Threadline runs everything around those inputs.
5. **The Authority Workshop.** Expertise and market signals become one root idea, the right expressions, distribution, response and learning.
6. **One idea, the right expressions.** Platforms are prescribed components rather than packages the client must choose.
7. **Commercially valuable attention.** The route should end in human conversation and honest evidence, not vanity reach.
8. **Expected → actual → why → change → retest.** The return loop is the core strategic difference.

## Positioning rules

Threadline is a managed authority and commercial-learning system for specialist expert-led firms. It is not presented as a LinkedIn agency, ghostwriter, video-production service, generic content agency, self-serve SaaS product or AI-automation agency.

The emotional value matters alongside the commercial value: respected expertise should be visible, recognised and associated with the firm. The site can speak to category standing and perception without pretending that status can simply be purchased.

The client experience stays simple. The public site sells the outcome and mechanism; it does not expose every operational detail in the client portal or overwhelm the buyer with internal architecture.

## Homepage length

Keep the front page to one clear narrative:

1. proposition and visual mechanism
2. expertise visibility gap
3. market memory
4. founder burden
5. Authority Workshop
6. expression, commercial route and learning
7. fit and action

Detailed engagement cadence, comparison tables, methodology and qualification belong on `/how-it-works`, `/who-its-for` and `/playbook`.
