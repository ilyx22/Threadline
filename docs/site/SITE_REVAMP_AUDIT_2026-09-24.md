# Site revamp and product audit — 24 September 2026

## Direction

The homepage now presents Threadline as a managed authority and commercial-learning system for expert-led B2B firms. Its eight-part story covers the proposition, expertise visibility gap, market memory, founder burden, Authority Workshop, expression and commercial route, learning loop, and fit.

The visual system uses editorial typography, restrained colour and tactile code-native objects. Motion explains cause and effect: expertise becomes a thesis, native expressions create buyer signals, and learning returns to the next cycle. Mascot-like characters and decorative motion are absent; illustrative material is labelled.

## Competitive review

The review covered the current public positioning of [Invisible Keyboard](https://www.invisiblekeyboardai.com/), [Rethoric](https://www.rethoric.com/), [Frontier](https://www.frontier-studio.com/), [Understory](https://www.understoryagency.com/linkedin-content), and [Contentreach](https://www.getcontentreach.com/).

The repeated strengths were a clear category, visible operating process, specific fit, and proof close to the claim. Threadline should not borrow their LinkedIn-first positioning or outcome promises. Its stronger point of difference is the complete operating loop: intelligence, creation, approval, production, distribution, evidence, attribution, and learning in one engagement system.

## Information architecture

- The homepage now explains the category and system in one pass.
- The detailed illustrative engagement lives on `/how-it-works`.
- Full fit and comparison detail remains on `/who-its-for`.
- Deeper methodology remains in `/playbook`.
- Application and qualification remain on `/apply`.
