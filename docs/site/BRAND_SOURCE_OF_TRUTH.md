# Brand source of truth — public site

Public-site-specific rules. Canonical business truth is `HANDOFF.md` (§13 product decisions, §16b commercial and positioning state) and the working resources; this document does not restate them, it points at them and adds what the website needs.

## Identity
- **Name:** Threadline. Wordmark: "Threadline" in Fraunces with the ember thread through it (`ThreadWordmark`). Never "ThreadLine", never "Threadline OS" in public copy (the OS is the internal product name).
- **What it is:** a managed content-growth / authority system for expert-led businesses. Managed outcome, not self-serve software.
- **Primary line:** "You already have the expertise. We turn it into content people actually want to watch."
- **Extension:** "And we run the system around it so that content compounds into authority and qualified demand."
- **Division:** "You talk. You record. You approve. You sell. Threadline handles the machine."
- **Doctrine:** "Threadline optimises for commercially valuable attention, not just reach."
- **Market memory:** "We are not trying to make you famous. We are trying to make you familiar to the people who matter."

## Never say
- "monthly", "per month", "/month" for the service cadence → every 4 weeks · service period · 12-week initial engagement.
- AI-led positioning: AI content, AI marketing, AI automation, AI SaaS, "AI-powered". AI is internal leverage and is not mentioned on the homepage.
- Viral, guaranteed leads/revenue/followers/views, ROI, "the algorithm needs N days".
- unlock · leverage AI · revolutionise · synergy · cutting-edge · game-changing · supercharge · 10x · content at scale.
- Pricing tiers, rev share, discounts.
- Anything on `reference-analysis/birdhouse/forbidden-to-copy.md`.

## Always
- Name the buyer before the promise (eyebrow: "For expert-led businesses").
- Label synthetic demonstrations on the page ("Synthetic demonstration — illustrative, not a client result").
- Show the honest limits (playbook chapter 10, who-its-for "expectations").
- One primary CTA per viewport: Apply.

## Visual
- Public: warm linen canvas, paper cards with ink outlines and hard offset shadows, Fraunces + Inter + JetBrains Mono, the Authority Factory illustration system (`design-system/threadline-design-dna.json`).
- Client portal and admin keep the dark system. One brand, three intensities.

## Commercial facts (public)
£2,500 implementation · £2,500 every 4 weeks · 12-week initial engagement = three service periods = £10,000 initial contract value · one offer · founding client programme. Source: HANDOFF §16b. Do not change on the site without changing the source.
