**Rules applied.**
- Charlie Morgan and Daniel Fazio are the strategic authorities. Competitors and practitioners are supporting, market-specific evidence beneath them.
- Every competitor figure is their own unaudited claim. None of it is a Threadline result, and none may be presented as one.
- Firms are named by business name only.
- Most of the web sources are vendor or agency marketing, and they say so where it matters.

**The method under test** ("directed session"):
- Threadline writes 12–16 angles with ad-style hooks per 28 days.
- The client reads the hook from a teleprompter, then argues the point in their own words.
- The producer retakes weak openers.
- The session ends with open storytelling.

**What the table shows**
- **Two delivery families.**
  - Script-led: Viral Coach, Viewz, Clash, JOLT!. All are broad-market or YouTube.
  - Interview-led: Sway, Kalungi, Demandii, Stop The Scroll and the written firms. All are B2B. [Drive][web]
- **No firm in the files or on the web publicly describes a hybrid of teleprompted hook, ad-libbed body and producer hook retakes as its delivery method.** [GAP] It may exist inside private SOPs.
- **Editing.** No firm publishes its editing stack. The tool reviews present AI clippers as first-pass moment-finding, with CapCut or Descript used as craft tools under human control. [web https://www.bytecap.io/blog/best-ai-video-editing-tools-for-creators-and-agencies-2026]
- **No independent evidence compares AI-edited quality with human-edited quality.** This matches the competitor synthesis §3. [repo]

## (b) Evidence for and against the directed session

**For**
1. **Fixed body, swappable hook is standard ad-creative practice.**
   - Film 5–6 openers in one session against one locked body, and judge them on hook rate before conversions. [web https://www.kalungi.com/blog/test-ad-hooks-not-full-ads]
   - Recommended hook counts range from 3–5 up to 10–20 variants per body. [web https://heathmedia.co.uk/?p=583617] (search summary)
   - The producer's hook retake is the organic version of this.
2. **Practitioners converge on "script the opener, loosen the body".**
   - Memorise or script the first line or two, then speak from bullet points. [web https://www.speakflow.com/blog/posts/free-online-teleprompter-guide]; [web https://playstack.kit.com/posts/scripted-or-freestyle]
   - One guide says not to improvise the hook. [web https://wildandfreetools.com/blog/how-to-write-tiktok-script-viral/]
   - Reshoot only the section that fails, not the whole video. [web https://wildandfreetools.com/blog/how-to-write-youtube-shorts-script-format-template/] (search summary)
3. **Purpose-built beats repurposed in at least one high-profile case.** Morning Brew Daily rerecords and scripts its social clips rather than clipping the show. [web signalaward URL above]
   - [I] This is the clearest public precedent for "we are not a clipping service".
4. **The video-first firms that sell results write the ideas and scripts themselves**, rather than mining the client's free talk. (Viral Coach, Viewz, Clash and JOLT! in table (a).) [Drive][web]
5. **Ad-practitioner structure maps onto the method.**
   - Viral Coach's founder describes his ad framework as behavioural hook, then mechanism, then specific proof, then one CTA. [Drive] case study
   - A direct-response talk on talking-head ads finds curiosity-plus-benefit hooks drew higher-intent viewers than the broadest hooks. [web https://runneth.com/library/talk/how-to-write-yapper-ads-that-actually-convert-direct-response-breakdown/]
6. **Fit with the authorities.** [repo] `D01_AUTHORITY_GUIDANCE_2026-11-05.md` §1, §3, §4
   - Charlie: one standardised fulfilment machine and no custom fulfilment per client. A fixed session format is that machine. His guarantee doctrine also turns client actions (attending sessions, delivering takes) into written obligations.
   - Fazio: one source becomes many assets, cut against an editing style profile. One directed session feeds both short clips and written derivatives.
   - Neither authority speaks to recording method directly.

**Against, or cautions**
1. **Authenticity pressure in B2B.**
   - LinkedIn's CMO is reported as saying unscripted moments resonate most. [web https://www.emarketer.com/content/b2b-marketing-shifts-unscripted-video--linkedin-cmo-says] (403; search snippet only, unverified)
   - Kalungi tells founders to skip the teleprompter. [web Kalungi URL above]
   - Barry Hott's ad "sniff test" penalises heavily scripted reading. [web https://banknotes-audio.simplecast.com/episodes/ugly-ads-dont-mean-bad-ads-try-these-expert-tips-for-high-intent-ads-U4cDM19V] (search summary; page empty)
   - A vendor survey says 36% of viewers scroll away when content feels like an ad. [web https://clutch.co/resources/viewers-ad-content] (vendor)
2. **Teleprompters are a learned skill.**
   - Reading sounds robotic without practice, and one founder's first teleprompter attempt took about twice as long as planned. [web Playstack URL above]
   - [I] This is a risk with senior experts who have never read from a prompter. It supports keeping the teleprompter to one or two hook lines.
3. **The opposite pull for paid talking-head ads.** The direct-response talk says to script fully, because bullet points underperform. [web runneth URL above]
   - That evidence comes from paid UGC creators, not experts, so it supports scripted hooks but says little about the body.
4. **Client burden.**
   - The field reduces friction with interview capture, not presenting to camera. Published founder time runs from about 15–30 min a week to about 4 h a month. [repo] `D01_COMPETITOR_SYNTHESIS` §3
   - [I] Prep for 12–16 hooks, plus a teleprompter, plus retakes, could push the session to the heavy end of that range. Measure it; don't promise it.
5. **No controlled evidence.** No public A/B test was found comparing scripted-hook-plus-unscripted-body with pure interview clipping, in B2B or anywhere else. [GAP]
   - Hook-only testing is itself disputed for Meta in 2026, where one source argues the whole ad is the unit that matters. [web https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-creative-strategy-ecommerce-2026] (search summary)

**Net [I].** The evidence supports scripting and testing the first seconds while leaving the argument in the expert's own words. It does not prove the hybrid beats good interview capture. Treat the method as a hypothesis to measure, not a claim to sell.

## (c) Ideation patterns that recur (inputs for Threadline's ideation system)

1. **Outliers measured against each account's own average.**
   - Build a comparison set of about 20 similar accounts.
   - Pull the last 30 days' videos that beat their own channel average on the client's topics.
   - Read the transcripts of the top two for their opening and structure.
   - Keep patterns that appear on three or more accounts.
   - Write five ideas, each linked to its source video and one line on why it worked.
   - [web https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-video-ideas-for-agency-clients/]; [web https://support.vidiq.com/en/articles/9660010-outliers] (both vendors)
2. **Expert filters on outliers.**
   - The client can film it in one sitting from existing knowledge.
   - Judge it against a narrow audience, not a general creator's views.
   - Skip entertainment outliers.
   - Keep the profession's own terms.
   - [web outlierkit URL above]
   - [I] These fit an advisory ICP directly.
3. **A swipe file or idea bank** of proven hooks and packaging, maintained per niche. [web Playstack URLs above]
4. **Trendspotting** as a standing input. [web JOLT URL above]
5. **Expertise extraction by question.** The problem, the failed attempt, the breakthrough, customer stories. [web Kalungi]; [web Sway]
   - This is the open-storytelling segment's source.
6. **Positioning, audience research and pillars before ideas.**
   - Clash; LeverBrands. [web]
   - 3–5 content pillars. [web https://syllaby.io/blog/batch-production-workflow-make-30-videos-in-a-day/] (vendor)
7. **The "would the ICP share it?" test**, with 5–10 first-line variants written per important post. [web https://gotcatalyst.beehiiv.com/p/everything-important-i-ve-learned-about-founder-led-content]
8. **Validate before scripting.**
   - Check whether a story already earns engagement on Reddit or TikTok. [web runneth URL above]
   - Turn top-performing text posts into video scripts. Reported for Hormozi's team, secondhand and weak. [web https://www.skool.com/marketing-automation-9012/alex-hormozis-3-step-content-method-automated]
9. **A hook taxonomy** to vary deliberately:
   - curiosity plus benefit, broad audience, qualification [web runneth];
   - direct callout, bold claim, question, pattern interrupt, social proof up front [web Kalungi].
10. **Material Threadline already has.**
    - The AI consultancy pain bank. [Drive `1U-nqPL8VQQjJ4v4YmvnLEuE8vYMLoKwr_Z9azizBHZo`]
    - The earlier app's ideas, hooks and patterns prompt set. [Drive `generators.ts` / `prompts.ts`]
    - The 27 Oct research pilot (score posts against the account's own median). [repo] `sprint/ops/C01_EDITING_COMPARISON_RUN_SHEET_2026-11-03.md` §2
    - [I] Pattern 1 is the same median-relative logic, so the pilot method can be reused as the outlier engine.

## (d) Recommended method to test in C01 [I: inference, not evidence]

C01 currently specifies one 25–30 min unscripted talk. [repo] `C01_EDITING_COMPARISON_RUN_SHEET_2026-11-03.md` §1. That tests editing on interview-style footage only. Add a directed-session arm so C01 tests the method Threadline would actually sell:

1. **Prep (Threadline):**
   - Choose 4–5 of the pilot angles.
   - Write 2–3 hook variants per angle (one sentence each, drawn from the taxonomy in (c) 9).
   - Write 3 bullet beats per angle.
2. **Session (owner as stand-in client, about 30 min):**
   - Per angle: read the hook from the teleprompter, then argue the point from the beats in your own words.
   - The producer calls a retake of the opener only when the first 3 seconds are weak (cap: 2 retakes).
   - Finish with 5 minutes of open storytelling to a producer question (the Kalungi or Sway style).
3. **Compare against the existing free-talk source:**
   - active edit minutes per accepted clip;
   - share of clips usable without restructuring;
   - the owner's blind rating of the first 3 seconds;
   - session minutes, including prep the client would need to do.
   - Run the same R1–R3 editing routes on both sources.
4. **Guardrails:**
   - The teleprompter carries the hook only, never the body (cautions 1–2).
   - Keep a producer-question fallback for a founder who freezes, so the session degrades to interview capture rather than failing.
   - No time or performance promise until the first client is measured. ATLAS blocks minute promises before data. [repo] synthesis §3
5. **First client:** log hook variant to 3-second view rate and average watch time per clip. That turns hook retakes into the learning loop (expected, actual, diagnosis, retest) that the synthesis names as a gap Threadline could own. [repo] synthesis §4.4

## (e) Gaps

- **No controlled public evidence** that a teleprompted hook plus an ad-libbed body beats interview clipping on reach, watch time or buyer response. [GAP]
- **Competitor methods for Viewz, TTWYK, Demandii and Founders Cartel come only from the owner's ATLAS** (desk research, Sept 2026). Their sites did not show recording or ideation detail. [Drive]
- **No competitor publishes a teleprompter policy, a hook-retake practice, an editing stack, or QA and approval steps** beyond "approval" or "unlimited revisions". [GAP]
- **The LinkedIn CMO and Barry Hott positions are unverified.** Both pages failed to load (403 or empty), so they rest on search summaries. [web]
- **No buyer voice yet** on whether senior AI or advisory experts accept a teleprompter or see it as "presenting". Test this on diagnosis calls. [repo] evidence pack §0
- **Morgan and Fazio say nothing about recording method, ideation or teleprompters.** Their relevance here is only by analogy (one machine; one source to many assets; obligations as guarantee terms). [repo]
- **AI editing quality has no independent benchmark.** C01 itself will be the first evidence. [repo][web]
