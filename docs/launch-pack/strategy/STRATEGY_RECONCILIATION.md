# Strategy reconciliation: software and pack against current Drive strategy (26 September 2026)

The canonical sources are listed in `../SOURCE_REGISTER.md`. The public frontend is frozen: owner decisions override Drive on anything that touches it.

## 1. Positioning, market, offer (confirmed)

- **Category:** a managed founder-authority / qualified-demand system ("managed authority-and-demand service, not a content factory and not SaaS access").
  - Threadline OS supports delivery and is not sold on its own.
  - Platforms are components, not the category.
- **Beachhead (a hypothesis, configurable):** founder- and partner-led AI transformation, AI strategy, fractional CAIO and specialist AI advisory firms, initially in the UK and US. "Expert-led B2B" is the umbrella, not a validated niche.
- **Offer:**
  - £2,500 implementation plus £2,500 every four weeks.
  - The initial engagement is 12 weeks: three 28-day service periods, £10,000 in total.
  - One offer: no tiers, revenue share or free trial.
  - The implementation fee is never sold as a standalone purchase.
  - Never say "monthly".
  - Pricing is not public.
- **Code:** the offer template and frozen engagement terms match (ledger ENG-02).
- **Scope:**
  - ~12–16 core short-form source assets per period, as a V1 hypothesis. Derivatives do not count towards it.
  - Up to ~3 channels.
  - Video-led where useful, with a text-led fallback.
  - Long-form YouTube is a pilot or custom item.
  - Excluded unless agreed: ads, funnels, email marketing, appointment setting, web development, daily community management, unlimited revisions.
- **Identity:** "Use a Threadline-led identity initially. Do not create a fabricated founder." Founder-personal commercial activity waits for the owner's employer clearance.

## 2. Research gate (code change kept; owner review requested)

**Drive position:**
- 10+ interviews, the first 5 interim, and more than 5 converging.
- "Only after qualitative convergence run Commercial Response Validation".
- The 26 Sept TODO adds: "Interviews/response validation are evidence to collect, not a reason to postpone truthful contact."

**The owner's 18A instruction (chat)** was that the historical rule must not block outreach, discovery or paid pilots, and that commercial tests may run with the uncertainty recorded.

**Implemented:**
- An early move from interviews to a commercial test needs a written uncertainty note, which is stored, shown and audited.
- "Validated" still needs the full sample.
- A paid pilot is **not** a free trial (no free trial exists).

**Status:** implemented and tested. Owner review requested, because it goes further than the Drive wording; see decision D-06 in `../OWNER_DECISIONS.md`.

## 4. Cadence, channels, PESTO

- **Cadence:** the in-app default is 3 pieces a week, editable per client. Drive forbids hard-coding "three daily posts/300 monthly posts". A weekly figure is kept off client material.
- **Channels:** LinkedIn outbound is the launch acquisition channel. Do not launch two unmeasured primary channels at once.
- **PESTO:** Personal, Expertise, Social proof, Trending, **Opinions**, plus objection and decision content. It comes from the Marcos Ruiz transcript (owner-confirmed). It is weighted to the client's evidence and never invented. The prompt and graphic 04 are corrected.

## 5. Marcos Ruiz (Birdhouse) and Thomas Murray (Vantage) lessons (hypotheses)

- **Content and outbound:** outbound creates predictable conversations quickly (Vantage), and content compounds authority (Marcos).
- **Funnel measurement:** first touch → reply → positive → booked → attended → qualified → proposal → won → cash, plus targeted and touches. The Acquisition page implements this.
- **Content-assisted pipeline** is tracked separately from directly attributable pipeline (the demand source, with evidence).
- **Follower-to-call** is "a diagnostic, not the north star", with no benchmark.
- **Do not copy** Vantage's rented-account model.
