# Threadline offer brief: short-form video for founder-led B2B experts

**13 October 2026. For owner decision. Nothing here is approved, public or contractual until the owner decides and a solicitor reviews the guarantee terms.**
Builds on: `THREADLINE_12_WEEK_PERFORMANCE_GUARANTEE_DESIGN_2026-09-27.md` and `THREADLINE_VIEWZ_GG_COMPETITOR_RESEARCH_2026-09-28.md` (Drive research folder), `STACK.md`, and the owner's 28 September notes.

## 1. The offer in one line

> **We turn the expertise in your head into short-form video that your buyers actually watch. You talk for an hour every two weeks; we do the rest, and we put a view guarantee behind it.**

- **Who it's for:** founder-led B2B expert firms. AI and technology advisory firms stay the lead segment (GTM docs), and the offer works for any expert-led B2B firm.
- **What they get:** short-form vertical video, cut from their own recorded conversations and edited to performance-ad standard (hooks, pacing, captions).
- **Where it goes:** the client's own accounts on **LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok** (agreed at kickoff; LinkedIn stays in because that's where B2B buyers are).
- **The price is unchanged:** £2,500 implementation plus £2,500 every four weeks; 12-week initial engagement; £10,000 in total.

## 2. Why this, and not multi-platform "diagnose what fits"

| | Multi-platform, diagnosed per client | **Short-form video, one production line** |
|---|---|---|
| Easy to sell | Abstract ("we'll diagnose") | Concrete ("short-form video, guaranteed views") |
| Easy to deliver and train | Custom every time | Same steps every client: an SOP you can hand to an editor |
| Plays to the owner's edge | No | **Yes: years of making ads (hooks, retention, editing)** |
| Guarantee possible | Hard (different metrics per platform) | **Yes: one counted metric (platform-reported views)** |
| Camera-shy founders | Covered by text | Mostly covered: clips come from a relaxed recorded conversation, not "filming content" |

**Diagnosis stays the differentiator, aimed at *what to say*:** the buyer's real problems, the founder's strongest opinions and stories, and which hooks earn attention from the right people. It no longer chooses the platform.

## 3. The production line (each four-week period)

1. **Record** two 60–90 minute conversations (Riverside or Meet). Threadline interviews the founder from the research-led question bank.
2. **Transcribe** (AssemblyAI, a few pence per hour) into the portal Library.
3. **Pick moments** (OpusClip suggestions plus the owner's hook judgement): **12–16 clips** per period.
4. **Edit** to ad standard (Descript / CapCut): a hook in the first 2 seconds, captions, pacing, a clear end.
5. **Client approves** in the portal (two-working-day window; the existing approval flow).
6. **Publish** natively to the agreed accounts (native schedulers; no growth tools).
7. **Measure and report:** views per platform (paid and organic shown separately) **plus** commercial signals: profile visits, DMs and enquiries (the existing performance and attribution pages).
8. **Next batch** leans into what worked: the learning loop, which is how ad testing already works.

Each step is a checklist. Steps 2–4 can be outsourced to a trained editor once the owner has set the standard.

## 4. The guarantee: three options

All three use the existing design's definition: **combined platform-reported views of Threadline-produced, approved, published videos on the client's agreed accounts, within 12 weeks**, conditional on four fair client commitments (kickoff inputs, attending recordings, approving within two working days, keeping accounts and posts live). Paid amplification is allowed, disclosed and reported separately, and paid for by Threadline.

| Option | Headline | If missed | Max cost to Threadline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Full refund** (existing design) | "100,000 views in 12 weeks or your money back" | Refund all fees (up to £10,000) | £10,000 plus work already done | The strongest hook and the biggest risk. |
| **B. Extension, then refund** | "100,000 views in 12 weeks, or we keep going free for another 4 weeks, then refund" | The client chooses: an immediate refund, or one free 28-day period and then a refund if still short | One period's delivery cost **plus** £10,000 | More generous. It must say the extension is **one** period, not "until it works". |
| **C. Pay per view delivered** (proportional) | "We refund 10p for every view short of 100,000" | Refund = £10,000 × the shortfall | Scales with the miss | No cliff at 99%, but a weaker hook. |

**The key maths for the owner:** paid video views on short-form platforms often cost around **1–3p each** (the owner's ad experience is the authority here; check real cost per view on B2B creative). If organic gets 60,000 and paid covers the last 40,000 at 2p, that's **about £800**, far less than a £10,000 refund. **Allowing disclosed paid amplification is what turns a scary guarantee into one you can underwrite.**

**Recommendation:** **Option A with 100,000 views**, *only after* a quick underwriting check: run £50–100 of paid views on 3–5 Threadline-made B2B clips and measure the real cost per view. If it comes in around 3p or less, 100,000 is safe to sell. If not, lower the number (e.g. 50,000) rather than weaken the remedy. A smaller number with a real refund beats a big number with a soft one.

*(This replaces my earlier "we keep working free until" suggestion: the existing design is right that an open-ended free-work promise is risky and can mislead.)*
