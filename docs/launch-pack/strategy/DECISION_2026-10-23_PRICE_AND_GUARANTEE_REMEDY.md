## Decided

**D03, price and payment timing:** keep £10,000 for the 12-week initial engagement: £2,500 implementation + 3 × £2,500 per 28-day period. Implementation is paid at signature. Each period is invoiced 14 days before it starts and is due by its first day. No work on a period before it is paid. (Amounts exclude VAT; the solicitor confirms the terms wording, F05/F09.)

**D04, guarantee remedy shape:** **C, proportional shortfall refund.** Refund R = P × max(0, 1 − V/T), capped at P (T = target, V = eligible counted views, P = stated fee at risk). At T = 100,000 and P = £10,000: 50,000 views → £5,000; 90,000 → £1,000; zero → £10,000. Chosen over A (full refund on any miss) and over no guarantee.
