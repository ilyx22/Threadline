## 4. The recording workload: evergreen audio, not bespoke videos

**Source (Stimuli Configuration):** "automate recording using an evergreen audio file, so all we need to focus on here is getting the script and delivery right." Audio length "1 min -> 2 min (no longer)"; three script variants, each recorded 3 times, best take kept. At scale: "Recording 500 looms per day with VA's or on canva is possible, however problems will start to arise", with a recommendation of the "Pitchlane Loom System". Per-prospect manual steps in Sending Looms: "Open the Loom > Add today's date in title > Click Share > Copy GIF Thumbnail > Paste into email."

**Correction:** LEAD_GEN_SYSTEM's "Looms (~95 × 3 min) | 285" minutes a day and TEST_PLAN's "about 3 minutes each to record" assume bespoke recordings. That is Threadline's own assumption, not the method. Replace it with: evergreen audio recorded once (3 variants), automated video assembly per prospect, plus the per-prospect handling steps above. **Minutes per prospect are UNKNOWN; measure them on the first 20.**

**⚠️ Conflict with Threadline's honesty rules:** CAMPAIGN_PACK says Looms are "Recorded **for this prospect**… no reused recording presented as personal" and bans "any trick to hide that a Loom was pre-recorded". **Proposed reconciliation (owner decision):** adopt the evergreen-audio method over prospect-specific visuals, and never claim or imply the narration was recorded live or personally for them. No "I recorded this just for you". That keeps the course's efficiency and Threadline's no-deception rule.

- **Counting rules (Alchemy Metrics, adopted):** primary metrics are counted "against the initial number of cold emails sent". Count "1 view per Loom sent". Attribute every event to the initial send date. A positive reply is anything except "not interested" or "remove me".
- **Correction:** TEST_PLAN's LVR "÷ Looms sent… arm B from step 3" drifts from this. Primary metrics must use the original first-touch cohort for both arms; stage-level rates are secondary.
- "If ABR is in KPI… don't change them, just increase the volume" (Loom OS): protect a working configuration.

## 6. The campaign test is confounded, so fix it before sending

**Source:** "This testing process will only work if you iterate one variable at a time."

CAMPAIGN_PACK's test ("Loom in email 1 versus Loom in follow-up 2") changes **three** things at once: the Loom's position, email 1's body ("Loomless body, soft CTA… no link"), and a link in email 1 (a deliverability effect). The result can't be attributed to placement.

**Two honest options (owner decision):**
1. **Baseline first (recommended):** run one standard configuration (Loom in email 1, per the course) until its metrics are readable; test placement afterwards as a single variable.
2. Keep the two-arm test, but **label it a comparison of complete sequences**, not a placement test.

The "1.5× bookings and at least 4 more" winner rule is Threadline's own, not a sourced rule; keep it labelled as such.
