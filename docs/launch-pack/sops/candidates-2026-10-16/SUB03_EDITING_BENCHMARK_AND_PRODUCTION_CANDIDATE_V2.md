## B. Fifteen proposed quality checks
One editing standard. It reconciles the economics document's acceptance bar (GUARANTEE_ECONOMICS_AND_TESTS_2026-10-15_V2, Part 2: "hook in the first 2 seconds; clean audio; readable captions; purposeful pacing; restrained cuts and punch-ins; correct facts; makes sense without the full conversation; brand treatment consistent") and the historical editing criteria (ED3: authority/brand fit, retention support, captions/reframing, pacing, B-roll, correction/QA minutes, cost, turnaround, revisions, reliability) with the technical, permission and approval checks below. Router doctrine applies: standard production is AUTHORITY-FIRST / HIGH-RETENTION / LOW-NOISE; heavy creator-style editing is optional premium intensity, and standard editing is never framed as basic. Time, cost, turnaround, revisions and reliability are recorded in A5 and A7, not scored here.

**Gating checks 1, 2, 3, 11 and 12:** any failure means REVISION or REJECT, whatever the styling or hook score.

1. **Meaning:** retains the speaker's intended point and necessary qualifications.
2. **Factual accuracy:** names, numbers, examples and technical terms checked against source.
3. **Claims/permissions:** no unapproved guarantee, proof, story, private information or fabricated result.
4. **Audience relevance:** opening and payoff address the intended buyer; edits do not create a misleading hook.
5. **Structure:** coherent opening/body/payoff; no missing context that changes meaning.
6. **Audio:** intelligible, consistent listening level, no distracting clipping/noise; inspect actual export.
7. **Captions:** correct words, timing, spelling and readable placement.
8. **Visual framing:** clear subject, useful cuts/B-roll, no misleading visuals or unnecessary effects.
9. **Brand/voice:** agreed style and natural delivery, consistent brand treatment, not generic agency template.
10. **Destination readiness:** correct agreed format, crop, safe placement and export profile verified for selected destination.
11. **Rights/access:** permitted media/music/fonts, client-safe access and no confidential material accidentally exposed.
12. **Delivery integrity:** correct root/version/file, plays completely, caption/media match, owner approval attached.
13. **Hook strength:** the opening earns the intended buyer's attention within the first 2 seconds (economics acceptance bar), without a misleading or unsupported hook. Score 1–5 as in the economics dry run and record why.
14. **Retention structure:** each beat earns the next; the clip makes sense without the full conversation; the payoff lands before attention is lost; no dead air or padding a viewer would skip. Judge the actual export, not the transcript.
15. **Pacing:** purposeful pacing matched to the content and speaker; restrained cuts and punch-ins; captions, B-roll and reframing support comprehension rather than add noise.

Score PASS / REVISION / REJECT with reason, plus hook strength 1–5 and "would publish Y/N" (the economics dry-run fields); no unsupported universal loudness, bitrate or subtitle specification. Tool/platform-specific export settings require verified current requirements and actual playback checks.

## C. Repeatable production
1. Receive original, approved brief/source permission, target versions and deadline; verify complete upload/playback.
2. Transcribe where needed; correct names/numbers and mark uncertain words against source. Do not fabricate unclear speech.
3. Select segments preserving meaning, source in/out time and root lineage. Confirm intended content job and CTA.
4. Produce first pass using selected route and brief. Keep source originals unchanged; separate project, draft and approved exports.
5. Operator runs the fifteen checks; fix internal issues before client review. Record changed claims needing renewed approval.
6. Deliver one consolidated approval batch with version-specific notes. Record approved/rejected state and requested revisions.
7. Revise against feedback, log reason/time and repeat QA. Do not overwrite the last approved version or assume approval transfers to a new cut.
8. After approval create final media/caption/thumbnail package and destination instructions; hand off to publishing with approval/version and IDs.
9. Verify actual published representation and capture live URL through publishing route; an export is not publication.
10. Update time/cost/revision ledger. Repeated more-than-two revision cycles trigger brief/quality/scope diagnosis under SOP 06.
