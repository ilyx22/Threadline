## 6. Draft script (45-75 seconds)
Most AI governance frameworks I review are very good at one thing: deciding whether something gets deployed. Almost none of them say what happens when you need to go backwards.

That is the gap I call reversibility. Governance teams are built to approve, not to unwind. The mental model is a gate. But once an AI system is live, its footprint spreads beyond the system itself, into decisions, data and the processes people have rebuilt around it. Switching the model off does not undo any of that.

So reversibility needs a plan for the footprint, not just the system.
