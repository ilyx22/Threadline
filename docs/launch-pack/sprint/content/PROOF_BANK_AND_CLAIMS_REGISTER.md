# Proof bank and claims register

**Status: drafted; owner review pending.** Prepared 26 September 2026 (TODO_AUDIT TC3 / C9).

This turns `claims/CLAIMS_AUDIT.md` (a report) into a register. It lists every claim Threadline might make, from the public site, the Drive content pack, the sales scripts and the drafted profiles, with its evidence class and status. It ends with an empty proof bank for client proof, with permission fields.

**Public-site claims are listed as "reported; owner decision".** The site is frozen and owner-approved. Nothing here recommends editing it. The status given for a site claim applies only to **reuse elsewhere** (posts, profiles, sales, email).

---

## Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E1 Measured** | Tracked by a platform or by Threadline's own system, with a definition and a date range | A post's impressions from platform analytics |
| **E2 Client-reported** | A client's own statement or data, used with written permission | "We booked three calls from it", in the client's words |
| **E3 Founder track record** | The founder's work before Threadline, self-reported. Never a Threadline client result | "100m+ views" |
| **E4 Process / capability** | True by design. Verifiable in the product, an SOP or a contract | "Every asset traces back to one root idea" |
| **E5 Commercial term** | True if it matches the signed terms | "12-week initial engagement in three four-week periods" |
| **E6 Opinion / point of view** | Not a factual claim; defensible judgement | "Views are weak evidence without buyer response" |
| **E7 Illustrative** | An invented example, labelled as such where it appears | "14 profile visits · observed", stamped Illustrative |
| **E8 Estimate / forecast** | Not yet measured. At best a planning hypothesis | "About twenty minutes a week" |

Outcome evidence (E1 and E2) also carries an attribution label, from transcript audit §5:
- DIRECTLY_TRACKED
- BUYER_NAMED_CLIENT_ATTRIBUTED
- MULTI_TOUCH_INFLUENCED
- ASSOCIATED_CORRELATED
- QUALITATIVE_ONLY

**Statuses:**
- **usable**: may be stated as worded;
- **needs substantiation**: hold until the evidence is on file;
- **never use**: breaks a claims rule in BRAND_GUIDELINES §6;
- **reported; owner decision**: a public-site claim, recorded for information only.

## E. Never-use list (applies everywhere)

- **Guarantees.** Views, followers, leads, revenue, ROI, "inevitable" outcomes, or a platform learning period ("the algorithm needs N days").
- **Invented proof.** Case studies, logos, testimonials, team members, credentials, spokespeople, or a portal or product described as in client use before it is.
- **Pricing on public material.** Figures, "starting from", tiers, discounts, scarcity numbers.
- **"Monthly" or "per month"** for the service cadence.
- **A founder-time figure** before it is measured.
- **The founder's identity or track record** in company-led content, until D-05.
- **"AI-powered", "AI content", "AI marketing"**, and the banned words: unlock, leverage, revolutionise, synergy, cutting-edge, game-changing, supercharge, 10x, content at scale, viral.
- **Dead links.** No URL in a post, bio or video until it returns 200, logged out.

## F. Proof bank (empty template for client proof)

**Rules** (content engine "Proof density loop"; transcript audit §5; sales verbatim library §A–B and L):
- An entry exists only with **written permission** that names its scope.
- Permission to be interviewed is **not** permission to publish (SOP 04).
- One evidence record feeds every expression of it: case study, proof card, sales line, website, content. Repeating a weak claim across surfaces does not make it stronger.
- Keep measured facts, client-reported facts and inference in separate fields.
- A falling or null result is recorded as it is.
- If the client says no, respect it and do not pressure.

### F3. Checklist before any proof item is used

- [ ] Written permission is on file, and the scope covers this surface.
- [ ] The client approved the exact wording.
- [ ] The evidence class and attribution label are shown or recorded.
- [ ] The raw evidence was checked by a named person.
- [ ] Measured, client-reported and inferred parts are kept separate in the copy.
- [ ] No figure is rounded up, and no window is cherry-picked (a bad period stays visible if it is part of the window).
- [ ] If the proof is the founder's own track record (E3), it is labelled as such, and D-05 is cleared.
- [ ] The placement is logged in `placements_used`.
