3. **Employer clearance.** The LinkedIn rows need the Company Page, which needs clearance (`PROFILES.md` gate 1). If the page is not live, drop the LinkedIn rows and run the rest.
4. **Owner review.** Each item is marked approved, rewrite or reject (master to-do P1: "Review the content pack for positioning, factual claims, anonymity, tone and platform fit").
5. **Human edit and anti-slop pass on every item** (transcript audit §2, "AI sandwich"). No direct model output is published.

Why the cadence is this low:
- **The master to-do says to keep it modest.** P1 says: "Keep launch cadence modest and review-led: use the prepared batch, then increase only when live quality, ICP response and fulfilment capacity support it."
- **The high targets are hypotheses, not the starting point.** The content engine's locked targets (X at about 10–20 a day, LinkedIn at 2–4 a day) are treated as "stretch/test hypotheses governed by quality, account maturity, platform safety and commercial response" (master to-do P1). This calendar is deliberately far below them.
- **Adjust after the first live signal.** Change the cadence only at the day-7 and day-14 reviews, and only one variable at a time. Build toward a 7–14-day approved buffer only after that signal (master to-do P1).

**Posting times.** The owner picks slots that match the buyer's working hours (the current prospect batch is US-focused). Log the actual time of every post. Schedule only through native or authorised tools, and keep replies human.

**Links.** No post in this calendar carries a link. The profile carries the CTA. This keeps the calendar independent of the domain and avoids asking for action before trust exists.

**Four-part gate** (content engine, "The primary quality gate: four ingredients"):
- **A**: authority
- **C**: curiosity
- **O**: perceived outcome
- **I**: ICP relevance

Ratings:
- **✓**: passes as written.
- **~**: partial. It passes the gate, but tighten it in owner review.
- **✗**: fails. No scheduled item is rated ✗; the items that fail are in the flags table.

**How to read the ratings.**
- **Authority** here rests on credible reasoning and specific distinctions, never on proof. The company-led account holds no publishable result yet.
- **ICP relevance** is rated ~ wherever the item speaks to "B2B founders" or "expert-led firms" in general. That is the umbrella, not the wedge (BRAND_GUIDELINES §6).
