## C. Concrete doctrine

### C.1 Outbound volume and channels

**Choose one system and give it time:**
- "Choose ONE system to start"; commit for "the next 120 days"; "Don't try to run 4 systems at once unless you have a team" (Roadmap `1OgnSxoI…`).
- Give a system "2-4 weeks to be running at full capacity" (Cold Email Mastery Overview).

**Which system fits a high-ticket offer:**
- Loom OS is for "High-ticket offers ✔ Low-volume, High-impact leads".
- Loomless OS is for "Offers that are already dialled in" (Cold Email Mastery).
- Trojan DM OS (LinkedIn and Facebook) is "designed for high-ticket offers and low-volume, high-value leads" (`1yH4RvC3…`).

**Volume:**
- Loom email proof of concept is "100+ per day" over "2-8 weeks".
- "100 emails a day at a 1% ABR = 20 appointments a month = 3-5 clients/month" (Loom Alchemy Metrics `1Vdw1icoz…`).
- Manual system: "400 emails every single weekday", split "100 MAIN → 100 FUP1 → 100 FUP2 → 100 FUP3" (`1FH9_rgn…`).

**Per-inbox limits** (the files conflict):
- "never exceed the 20 limit per day" per account, and "We DO NOT increase the amount of emails we send per account" (`1oS-3Lmr…`).
- 5 cold plus 15 follow-up per inbox for Loomless (`1t4bHEn6…`).
- Maximum 2 inboxes per domain.
- Recommended providers: "only … Google Workspace & Outlook".

**Scale table:** 100/day needs 10 domains and 20 accounts; 500/day needs 50 domains and 100 accounts (`1oS-3Lmr…`).

**Screened niches:** if a niche has a gatekeeper or "screener", "you need to try another outbound system" (Loom Doctor).

**Other channels:**
- DM: go where the niche is active, and "After 6 weeks, assess … double down" (`1oLfE0MO…`).
- Calling: "At least 200 calls per day" per caller.
- Meeting format: "meetings over the phone beat zoom meetings on show up rate hands down" (Theory of Show Rate).

### C.2 List building and lead sources

**Build lists by hand first:**
- "The manual process … WILL get the best results". Scraped lists get "hundreds of daily emails from other marketing agencies" (`1mGXKP3W…`).
- Process: "Finding where you niche congregates > Finding an extraction point > Extracting the data".
- For agencies, coaches and consultants: LinkedIn, then the website, then Snov.io.
- Email finding order: "Email Scraper -> Ctrl-F relevant pages -> Google search -> Email permutator".
- Owner finding order: "Website > Google Search > Company Filing > Social Media".
- Quota: "100 leads for the day". "You can normally scale to 300 leads per day manually."
- Databases (Apollo, Listkit) are "a last resort" (`172lYWFD…`).
- Clay enrichment for first lines is "by far the most powerful angle".

**Verification and lead quality:**
- Double verification (NeverBounce and Million Verifier). Send only to "Valid".
- Files conflict on "accept all" and "info@". The FAQ says "No" to both, which is the stricter rule.
- Send to personal addresses ("charlie@").
- "We accept no more than a 2% bounce rate per sending account."
- Avoid a "'blasted' list".

**Sizing:** "Sourcing 100 leads will take a lead sourcer between 4-7 hours" (`1FcuvPPN…`).

### C.3 Offer and risk reversal

**What an offer is:**
- "A product/service/guarantee/discount/price is NOT an offer"; "Niches & markets don't saturate, offers do" (`1BTqLYVq…`).
- Formula: "(Niche - Outcome - Timeframe - Risk Reversal - Methodology)*Spin" (`1Oo6rIsH…`).
- A pitch is 1–2 pages, "understood in 2 minutes or less. The pitch does not include the price" (`16tn2NLz…`).

**Guarantees and refunds:**
- "All info products should have a full refund guarantee"; the outcome "MUST be tangible" (`1O7x8C4p…`).
- "Only ever put a refund on the line (never go into the red)" (`1ignw755…`).
- The "Red zone" (refund plus extra cash) is only for those with "300+ clients with proven results, an ultra low refund rate (2% or less)" (`1O7x8C4p…`).
- DFY/DWY: refund conditional "on YOU and THEM doing the work"; "Never free, no matter what" (`1Oo6rIsH…`).
- Guarantee terms act as the client's to-do list. Raise the required actions "minimum of 2x"; "nothing less than 6 months and nothing more than 12 months" (`1Tad…`).
- EasyGrow's own guarantee is 20 clients in 180 days, or a refund plus $5,000. It carries 10 conditions, including "500 outbound 'cold' approaches per day" (`1TMh4TY4…` term 3).

**Offer impact:** the offer "can move your sales conversion rate by 10-40%, your show up rate by 10-40%" (`1BTqLYVq…`).

### C.4 Messaging and copy frameworks

**Loom OS email body:** "hi (firstname) > Personalised first line > offer stated with conviction > risk reversal > loom link > further conviction or authority statement > request for call > thanks > (optional) p.s."; "Have 2-3 'conviction points'"; "Best copy = good offer!" (`1j5OoDz2…`).

**Loomless email:** "4 moving parts: Personalised first line - Offer / Who you help - Case study - Soft CTA". Example CTA: "Can I send a quick video explaining how it works?" Written at a "6th grade reading level" (`19H-Hrc2…`).

**Formatting and deliverability rules:**
- Subject "Question for (name/company)".
- "no calendar link in the first email"; no links or images in email 1.
- Plain text only: "HTML damages deliverability". Open and click tracking off.
- Spintax "50%+" of the first email.
- An opt-out line.

**Loom video:**
- "1 min -> 2 min (no longer)".
- Get "to the point & offer in the first 10-20 seconds".
- Record over the prospect's website and name the Loom after the prospect (Loom Doctor, Stimuli Configuration).

**Testing:** change one variable at a time ("Ceteris Paribus"). "If ABR is in KPI, change nothing." "Better to pump more volume into a 'sub-par' system than to try improving it" (Testing Flow `1VZUEj8i…`).

### C.5 Follow-up cadence

**Cold sequence:**
- 4 emails "over a 6-10 day period", spaced "every 2, 2 & 3 days (excluding weekends)" (`1FH9_rgn…`).
- Loom OS: "we'll follow up 3 times: 1 - bump; 2 - bump + loom; 3 - bump". Sentences "no longer than 15 words" (`1mi8nql5…`).

**Engaged (warm) prospects:**
- "every 2 days x 8"; after 8 unanswered attempts, label "Not Interested" (`1_DJsPQ_…`).
- "memes on your last 2-3", with the calendar link in each (`1yHkr2Zf…`).

**Calling:**
- "80% of successful sales take five or more follow-up calls" (Cold Calling Mastery).
- After a no-show, "call twice a day, every day for another 7 days" (`1Q35VAzC…`).

### C.6 Reply handling

**Labels:** "Not Interested, Engaged, Booked & No-Show". "All prospects should have one label and no more." Positive means "anything that isn't directly negative" (`1_DJsPQ_…`).

**Replying:**
- "DO NOT FALL INTO THE TRAP OF ANSWERING QUESTIONS. WE NEED CURIOSITY" (`1Dsfb4na…`).
- "we don't want to tell them how much it costs before the call". If pressed, "anchor the price directly to the result" (`1Dsfb4na…`, `1MB2…`).
- Don't reply to rude or "not interested" responses.
- Reply speed: "within 12 hours (ideally 6)". The Testing Flow says "ideally 1-6 hours".

**Post-reply "omnipresence" sequence:**
1. LinkedIn connection note: "I received your reply via email".
2. Other social channels.
3. "CALL THEM!", with a double dial and no voicemail.
4. Voice note or text (`1Dsfb4na…`).

### C.7 Booking and show rates

**Booking:**
- Offer "10+ slots available" and avoid "overqualifying".
- Give each system or setter its own booking event, so the source can be tracked (Loom Doctor).
- Booking window: "2-4 days"; "NEVER book someone more than 5 days out" (Theory of Show Rate).
- Availability: "at least 24 hours of availability every week" (Theory of Show Rate).

**Reminders:**
- One confirmation call 24 hours before; "do not make more than one confirmation call after someone confirms".
- "Are You Still Good For Our Call in An Hour?" sent "56 minutes before" (`1Q35VAzC…`).

**No-show protocol:** call at the start time; voicemail, SMS and email; call again at +5 and +10 minutes; then a reschedule request (`1Q35VAzC…`).

**Measuring:**
- "Get to 30 appointments and then you can take a proper look at your show rate."
- Expect no-shows "10-20% of the time" (`1_DJsPQ_…`).

### C.8 Sales call structure

**The call:**
- Five steps: "Line of questioning → Pitch → Answering their questions → Conquering objections → Closing".
- "Calls typically run for 45-60 minutes"; "aiming for a one call close"; "No more bullshit 'proposals'" (Sales Bedrock `1XEm…`).
- Phase lengths: questioning 20–40 minutes, pitch and Q&A 5–15, objections 2–10, payment 5–10 (`1hWk…`).
- Question flow: "current situation → problems → emotional impact → desired situation → consequence → urgency". Set the frame in "the first 30 to 60 seconds" (`1wMN…`).

**The close:**
- The close "starts the second you drop the price and ask, 'How do you feel?'"
- Clear objections in this order: "Re-Tie & Value, Money, Time, Partner, Fear".
- Payment concession order: pay in full, then 50% now and 50% in 30 days, then 3 payments, then 4–5, then a refundable deposit of about 10%.
- "The deal isn't done until the cash is collected." "Always Leave with a Decision" (The Close 1–9).

**Onboarding on the call:**
- "All onboarding steps are to be taken while on the call".
- "ALWAYS GET PAYMENT BEFORE SENDING TERMS"; next-day access (`1jCz…`).

### E.2 Prospect list method (`docs/launch-pack/sprint/prospects/`, currently empty)

Write the method as follows:
