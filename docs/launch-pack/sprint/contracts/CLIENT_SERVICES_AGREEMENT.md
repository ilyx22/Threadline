## 1. Definitions

- **Agreed Final Output**: a content asset, script, strategy document or report that Threadline has delivered to the Client and the Client has approved as final (or which is marked final in the Client's workspace).
- **Approval Window**: the period, stated in the Order Form, within which the Client's named Approver reviews and approves or requests changes to an item.
- **Background IP**: everything Threadline owned, licensed or developed before or independently of this Agreement, and everything of general application it develops while performing it, including: software (including Threadline OS and its workspace), prompts and prompt chains, model routing, templates, frameworks, research methods, scoring rubrics and evaluation ("Judge") logic, automations, SOPs, internal datasets, quality rules, and generalised, de-identified learnings drawn from across clients.
- **Client Materials**: anything the Client, or someone acting for it, provides to Threadline: recordings, footage, voice, likeness, text, brand assets, logos, proof, case material, customer and sales information and access to accounts.
- **Core Asset**: a distinct short-form source piece of content (typically a short video, or a text-led piece where text-led delivery has been chosen) built from one approved idea. Repurposed, reformatted or cross-posted versions of the same piece ("**Derivatives**") are not additional Core Assets.
- **Implementation**: the one-off installation work described in clause 3.1 and the Order Form.
- **Initial Term**: the first 12 weeks from the Start Date, made up of three Service Periods.
- **Installation Deliverables**: the deliverables defined in clause 7 (the Day-7 milestone).
- **Required Inputs**: the Client inputs listed in the Order Form as needed before, or for, Implementation.
- **Service Period**: a period of 28 consecutive days (four weeks). The first Service Period starts on the Start Date. Service Periods are **not** calendar months, and fees are **not** monthly. Thirteen Service Periods make up roughly one year.
- **Start Date**: the date in the Order Form on which Service Period 1 begins.
- **Working Day**: Monday to Friday, excluding bank and public holidays in England.

## 3. The Services

3.1 **Implementation.** Threadline will establish the Client's content system: positioning and core messages; the Brand Brain (voice, claims and evidence); a voice guide; workflow and approval routes; measurement and tracking; recording set-up; and the first researched scripts. Implementation begins once the fulfilment gate in clause 5.4 is met and aims to finish within the first seven days (see clause 7).

3.2 **Ongoing service in each Service Period.** Threadline will provide research and market intelligence; strategy; ideas, hooks and scripts; a recording queue and recording guidance; production coordination, editing, quality control and packaging; scheduling and publishing through connected accounts or by hand; performance and commercial-signal tracking; weekly optimisation; and a review and report at the end of each Service Period.

3.3 **Working scope, not a guarantee.** The V1 working scope is **approximately 12 to 16 Core Assets per Service Period**, repurposed into Derivatives where sensible, across **up to approximately three channels** agreed in the Order Form. This range is Threadline's current expected output, not a guaranteed quantity. Actual output depends on the Client recording, supplying inputs and approving within the Approval Window, and on the channel mix prescribed after diagnosis. Derivatives never count towards the Core Asset range. **[SOLICITOR: confirm "working scope" wording does not create a fixed-quantity obligation]**

3.4 **Format.** Delivery is video-led where video is the right format for the Client's market, with a text-led fallback (for example LinkedIn, X or Threads text) where it is not. The format mix is prescribed by Threadline after diagnosis and reviewed each Service Period.

3.5 **Editing standard.** Threadline's standard editing is shown to the Client before signature. Heavier creator-style or documentary-level editing is not included and is scoped and priced separately if requested.

3.6 **Long-form YouTube.** Long-form YouTube content is not part of the standard Services. It is available only as a separately agreed pilot or custom item in the Order Form or a later written variation.

3.7 **Exclusions.** Unless separately agreed in writing and priced, the Services do not include: paid advertising management; funnel building; email marketing; appointment setting; website or web development; daily community management (comments and direct messages remain the Client's, or a person the Client names); sales-team management; unlimited bespoke creative; or unlimited revisions. Out-of-scope requests are logged and may be quoted separately; they are not absorbed silently.

3.8 **Revisions.** Each item includes up to **{{revision_rounds, proposed 2}} rounds** of consolidated changes from the Approver. Further rounds, or changes to an item after it has been approved, count as new work and may reduce the number of new Core Assets in that Service Period. **[OWNER DECISION C-03]**

3.9 **Use of AI and tools.** Threadline uses software and AI tools, including third-party AI models, in delivering the Services. Every Agreed Final Output is reviewed by a person at Threadline before it is presented for approval. Nothing is published without the Client's approval (clause 4.3).

3.10 **Method and personnel.** Threadline decides how the Services are performed, including tools, providers and personnel, and may use subcontractors. Threadline remains responsible for their work.

## 7. Installation milestone (risk reversal)

> **[OWNER DECISION C-02: approve the milestone and choose the remedy.] [SOLICITOR: this must read as a service-level commitment with a defined, sole remedy, not a guarantee of results.]**

7.1 **The milestone.** Threadline commits to deliver the following **Installation Deliverables within 7 days of the Installation Start**:
- (a) **3 to 5 core messages or content pillars**, set out in writing for the Client's approval; and
- (b) **the first 6 to 8 researched scripts**, ready for the Client to review and record.

7.2 **Installation Start** means the later of (i) the day the fulfilment gate in clause 5.4 is met, (ii) the kickoff (Brand Brain) session taking place, and (iii) Threadline receiving the Required Inputs marked "needed for Day 7" in the Order Form.

7.3 **Conditions.** The milestone applies only if the Client attends the kickoff session, provides the Required Inputs, and responds to any questions Threadline raises during installation within 2 Working Days. Days lost to Client delay are added to the 7-day period.

7.4 **What the milestone measures.** It measures delivery of the Installation Deliverables to Threadline's standard quality bar. It does not depend on the Client liking every message or script; requested changes are handled as revisions under clause 3.8.

7.5 **Remedy.** If Threadline misses the milestone for reasons within its control, then, as the Client's sole remedy for that miss, Threadline will keep working on installation at no extra cost until the Installation Deliverables are delivered, **and** (choose one):

- **Option A (recommended): time extension.** Service Period 1 is extended at no charge by the number of days by which the milestone was missed, so the Client receives a full 28 days of service after installation.
- **Option B: credit.** A credit of **£{{credit_amount}}** is applied to the next Service Period invoice.
- **Option C: refund backstop.** If the Installation Deliverables have not been delivered within 14 days of the Installation Start, the Client may end the Agreement by written notice and receive a refund of the Implementation fee (and any Service Period fee paid for a period not yet started).

**Recommendation:** Option A, with Option C as a backstop only if the owner wants a stronger public promise. Option A is fully within Threadline's control and carries no cash exposure.
