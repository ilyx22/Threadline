## Expertise conversation (gap-focused)

Answer only from real experience. "I don't know" is a valid answer, and nothing is invented to fill it.

1. What do AI advisory buyers get wrong before they hire someone?
2. Tell me about a time you changed your mind about content or positioning. What changed it?
3. What's a common piece of advice in your market you think is wrong? Why?
4. Walk through how you'd judge whether a video is worth making.
5. What question would you most like a prospect to arrive having already answered?
6. What won't you claim, even though competitors do?
7. What have you actually seen work, and how sure are you?

## Metrics calculated at the end

Cost per accepted root and derivative package · owner burden · first-pass acceptance · rewrite minutes by reason code · recovery time · elapsed time to approved package. Subscriptions are allocated explicitly, including any already paid for.

## Hard fails (stop the run)

Invented proof · ineligible source leakage · wrong tenant or account · stale approval transferred · accidental posting · duplicate action · missing lineage · unusable export · synthetic results presented as real.

## Decision output

For each handoff: KEEP, FIX, TEST alternative or DEFER, with the evidence.

## Not adopted from the research

Creator guarantees, follower benchmarks, posting quotas, refundable deposits, paid retargeting, reminder barrages and installation-and-exit stay out of this run. Grok Bot, schedulers and the API integrations are separate capability tests.
