# Kickoff agenda (Brand Brain session, 60–90 minutes)

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing. Sources: SOP 04 ("Book 60–90 minute Brand Brain/kickoff"), Execution Manual V14.3 ("Brand Brain / onboarding 60–90 minutes"), the onboarding intake draft.

The kickoff is a conversation, not homework. Threadline prefills the Brand Brain from what you've sent; this session corrects and deepens it.

**How it runs:** before the call you only fill in your business basics and access. During the call Threadline has your onboarding open and types as you talk, so you never fill in the long form yourself. The call is recorded, and the transcript can be used to fill any gaps; every drafted answer is checked by a person before it's saved. Afterwards you get a notice to check it: answers we typed are marked **From your call**, you correct anything, then confirm, and your workspace is built.

| Part | What we cover | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Business and economics | What you sell, to whom, what a good client is worth, how you win work today, capacity for more | Commercial baseline and start date recorded |
| 2. Buyer and language | Best and worst-fit clients, trigger events, the exact words buyers use, objections, what they tried before | Buyer section of the Brand Brain |
| 3. Expertise and point of view | Opinions you'd defend, stories, mistakes you learned from, frameworks you use but haven't named | Beliefs, stories and proof sections |
| 4. Claims and proof | What you can prove, what you won't claim, what must never be used | Claims and proof limits |
| 5. Voice and editing style | How you sound, phrases you'd never say; your **Editing Style Profile**, with visual references and examples of standard Threadline editing | Voice guide and editing profile |
| 6. Ways of working | Approver and backup, recording cadence (if video is prescribed), how commercial signals reach us, measurement setup (see below) | Responsibilities confirmed |
| 7. Early win and next steps | One **early win** agreed in words; the first-period plan; what happens this week | Early win recorded; installation steps assigned |

**Measurement setup, agreed in the session:**
- map the journey from content to a conversation (profile → site → booking or enquiry);
- tracked links where the platform allows them;
- a "how did you hear about us?" question on your booking or enquiry route;
- where enquiries and deals are recorded (your CRM or a simple list);
- the baseline: where things stand today, before anything changes.

**Optional:** the success-interview question (see the [welcome guide](WELCOME_GUIDE.md#proof-only-if-you-want-to)), with your answer recorded as YES, MAYBE or NO.

After the session, you check and confirm the onboarding answers (about 10 minutes). Anything Threadline writes to the Brand Brain later shows as **waiting for your confirmation** until you confirm it.
