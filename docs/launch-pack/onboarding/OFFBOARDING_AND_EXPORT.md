# Offboarding and export

> Status: drafted; owner review pending. Client-facing, with retention an open owner decision.

## Exporting your data at any time

**Settings → Workspace → Your data → Prepare an export** (workspace admins only).

- The export is a JSON copy of everything recorded for the workspace: Brand Brain, ideas, scripts, content, approvals, reports, invoices, pipeline and time.
- Files are listed in the export but not included; download them from the **Library**.
- The download link lasts seven days.

## Ending the engagement

When an engagement ends, Threadline starts offboarding. That:

1. ends the engagement;
2. disconnects any connected platform accounts and deletes their stored access;
3. cancels queued work (nothing further is posted);
4. prepares the full export.

The download link appears on **This week** until access ends. When access ends, client members are suspended automatically.

You keep the content that was delivered to you. How ownership is worded is set out in your agreement.

## How long data is kept afterwards

> **Owner decision pending.** The offboarding form currently defaults to a 30-day export window and then keeping data for 90 days, but no retention period has been approved. The options are:
>
> - short retention (for example, 30 days after access ends);
> - the current default (90 days);
> - longer retention for evidence and dispute purposes.
>
> Each needs to match the privacy notice and the data processing agreement. Don't quote a period to clients until one is approved.

After the retention date, a Threadline super admin deletes the workspace. A legal hold prevents deletion. The offboarding record is kept as evidence.

## Before you leave

- Download the export and any Library files you want.
- If you gave any proof permissions (testimonial, case study, figures, logo), you can withdraw them in **Settings**. Withdrawing takes effect immediately.
