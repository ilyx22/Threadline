# Reporting, support and escalation

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing, with one owner decision (marked below).

## Reports (Reports)

- **Weekly report:** Threadline drafts it, checks it and marks it final, and each reader gets one email. It covers:
  - what shipped;
  - what we expected before each piece went out;
  - what happened;
  - what we’re testing next.

  If a final report needs correcting, you keep seeing the current version until the correction is final.
- **Four-week review:** at the end of each four-week period, written as **Action → Results → Problems → Future**, with the period’s figures computed from the records. Under those headings every review answers:
  - where we started; what was weak; what we changed; what happened; what we learned;
  - the current weakest link; the next intervention; our confidence; the next test.

  Each change is traced as **expected → actual → why → change → retest**.
- **PDF:** every final report opens as a PDF (**Download PDF**).
- **Results:** shows the evidence for each figure, labelled by how strongly it’s evidenced. We don’t claim a cause the evidence doesn’t support. The five classes, strongest first:
  1. **Directly tracked:** a tracked link, form or booking source ties it to a piece.
  2. **Buyer-named, client-attributed:** the buyer said which piece or channel brought them.
  3. **Multi-touch influenced:** the buyer saw several pieces on the way.
  4. **Associated / correlated:** it moved at the same time; no direct link.
  5. **Qualitative only:** a comment or impression, not a count.

  More in the [results and attribution guide](RESULTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION_GUIDE.md).

## What the first cycle looks like

The first four-week period is **Establish / Calibrate**. The first pieces are the first real data from your market. Before each piece goes out, we record what we expect it to do. After it’s out, we compare that with what actually happened, diagnose the gap, and change one thing at a time. A weak week is reported as a weak week. We don’t promise a set “learning period”.

## Getting help (Help)

- Anyone in the workspace can write **What do you need?** plus details, and press **Send to Threadline**.
- Tick **This is stopping us from doing something** if it’s blocking you. It then reaches the team as a blocking request.
- Your workspace’s requests show their status:
  - **Received**
  - **Being looked at**
  - **Waiting on something**
  - **Resolved** (with the resolution)

## Escalation

1. Raise it on **Help**, and mark it as blocking if it is.
2. If a blocking request hasn’t moved, contact `{{support_owner}}` at `{{support_contact}}`.
3. Commercial or contractual concerns go to the person who signed your agreement at Threadline.
