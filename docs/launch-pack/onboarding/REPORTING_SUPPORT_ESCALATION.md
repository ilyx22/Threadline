# Reporting, support and escalation

> Status: drafted; owner review pending. Client-facing, with one owner decision (marked below).

## Reports (Reports)

- **Weekly report:** Threadline drafts it, checks it and marks it final, and each reader gets one email. It covers:
  - what shipped;
  - what we expected before each piece went out;
  - what happened;
  - what we’re testing next.

  If a final report needs correcting, you keep seeing the current version until the correction is final.
- **Four-week review:** at the end of each four-week period, written as **Action → Results → Problems → Future**, with the period’s figures computed from the records.
- **PDF:** every final report opens as a PDF (**Download PDF**).
- **Results:** shows the evidence for each figure, labelled by how strongly it’s evidenced, from directly tracked through to qualitative only. We don’t claim a cause the evidence doesn’t support.

## What the first cycle looks like

The first four-week period is **establish / calibrate**. The first pieces are the first real data from your market. Before each piece goes out, we record what we expect it to do. After it’s out, we compare that with what actually happened, diagnose the gap, and change one thing at a time. A weak week is reported as a weak week. We don’t promise a set “learning period”.

## Getting help (Help)

- Anyone in the workspace can write **What do you need?** plus details, and press **Send to Threadline**.
- Tick **This is stopping us from doing something** if it’s blocking you. It then reaches the team as a blocking request.
- Your workspace’s requests show their status:
  - **Received**
  - **Being looked at**
  - **Waiting on something**
  - **Resolved** (with the resolution)

## Escalation

1. Raise it on **Help**, and mark it as blocking if it is.
2. If a blocking request hasn’t moved, contact your Threadline account lead directly.
3. Commercial or contractual concerns go to the person who signed your agreement at Threadline.
