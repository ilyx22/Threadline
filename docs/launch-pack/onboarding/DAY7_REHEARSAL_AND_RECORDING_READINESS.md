## Why rehearse

Day 7 is the first point at which a client judges whether the installation is real. The rehearsal checks three things on the real screens:
- that every milestone can be reached;
- that each one says honestly who it is waiting on;
- that nothing simulated looks like proof.

## C08: running the rehearsal (owner and Claude, about 2 hours plus waiting time)

1. **Staff:** complete milestones 2, 3 and 5. Time each one with a stopwatch (active minutes, plus waiting minutes listed separately).
2. **Client account:** open This week and the Installation page.
   - Does each milestone show the right owner?
   - Is the "what we're waiting on" line true?
3. **Client account:** sign off the strategy (milestone 4). Confirm staff can't sign off on the client's behalf.
4. **C12:** the recording setup check (below).
5. **Client account:** record the batch, mark it recorded and upload the footage (milestone 7).
6. **Staff:** assign an editor and move the pieces into production (milestone 8).
7. **Run the Day-7 demo as you would for a client, about 20 minutes**, using only real screens:
   - Installation page (what's done and what's next);
   - Brand Brain (theirs, confirmed);
   - Diagnosis (the named constraint);
   - Brief and strategy;
   - the scripts;
   - Recording Room;
   - the production board;
   - when the first weekly report is due.

   Say "demo mode" wherever demo output appears.
8. **Write down:** each milestone's minutes, every point where it got stuck, any wording that over-promises, and anything the client would have to ask about.

**Done (C08)** when all eight milestones are reached in the test workspace and the demo has been run once. The minutes per milestone are recorded, which feeds C05/C06 and the economics, and the defects found are either fixed or logged.

## C12: recording readiness and client responsibilities

**The setup check, tested live**
- [ ] **Client account:** submit a setup photo and a 30–60-second test-clip *link* on Recording → Your setup, then press Send for review.
- [ ] **Staff:** watch the clip and rate all seven checks (audio, light, framing, background, focus and stability, repeatability, format). Grade each one KEEP, MOVE, CHANGE or BUY.
- [ ] **Staff:** set an honest overall status: READY, READY WITH LIMITATION or BLOCKED, naming the single most important change.
- [ ] **Client account:** the result and the top change show on This week, and stay there until the issue is fixed.
- [ ] Nothing asks for an expensive studio. Upgrades are suggested in order (audio, light, framing, then camera), and buying anything is the client's choice.

**Responsibilities, recorded in the workspace**
