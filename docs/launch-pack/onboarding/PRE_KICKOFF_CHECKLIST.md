# Before your kickoff: the short list

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing. Source: SOP 04 Close to Kickoff (Drive), "minimal pre-kickoff request".

Send what you already have. Nothing here needs to be written specially, and anything you can't find can wait: say so and we'll note it as pending with a date.

- [ ] **Website:** the main site, and any page that explains what you sell.
- [ ] **Offer links:** service pages, proposals or one-pagers you already use.
- [ ] **Content channels:** the profiles and channels you post on now (or would like to).
- [ ] **Relevant proof:** results, case notes, quotes or recognition you can stand behind, and anything that must **not** be used.
- [ ] **Access method:** how we'll get access to the channels we publish to. We only use each platform's own delegation or connection. **Never send a password.**
- [ ] **Sales and customer sources, if available:** call recordings or notes, common questions, objections, emails where buyers explain what they need.
- [ ] **Your approver:** who approves content, and a backup for when they're away.
- [ ] **Who records:** who will be on camera or voice, if video is part of the plan.

Upload files in your workspace (**Content → Library**), or send links. Most of the business questions are in the in-app onboarding; this list doesn't repeat them.
