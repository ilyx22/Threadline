# Client FAQ

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing.

**How much of my time will this take?**
There's no fixed number. Onboarding, a kickoff conversation, recording sessions and approval passes are the core of it. Your team records the time spent on **Your time**, so we both see what it actually takes and can reduce it.

**Do I have to be on camera?**
Only where it adds trust. Formats are chosen for each idea.

**Which platforms will you post on?**
The ones your buyers actually use, decided after diagnosis. We can post through a connected account only once that platform's connection is set up and approved on our side. Otherwise we post by hand and record the link.

**Can anything be published without my approval?**
No. Every script, edit and packaging needs approval of that exact version. If it changes, it needs approving again.

**Can someone else approve for me?**
Yes. Give them the **Approver** profile under **Settings → People**.

**What if I don't like a draft?**
Use **Request changes** with a note. Comments stay attached to the version they were about.

**Will you make claims about my results?**
Only claims that are in your Brand Brain as cleared proof. Packaging with an unverified figure or promise language can't be approved.

**What results should I expect, and when?**
We don't guarantee views, leads or revenue. The first four-week period sets the baseline. By the third (Compound / Concentrate), the work concentrates on what your market has responded to. Every weekly report shows what we expected and what happened, even when the result is poor.

**How long is the engagement?**
The first engagement is twelve weeks, in three four-week periods. After that it continues four weeks at a time.

**How am I billed?**
According to your agreement. Invoices and signed agreements are under **Settings → Billing**, visible to admins and to anyone with the Commercial profile.

**Who owns the content?**
As set out in your agreement. Delivered content and your data can be exported at any time.

**Can my editor or assistant have access?**
Yes. Invite them as **Editor** (production only) or as a **Member** with the profile that fits.

**Is my data private?**
Your files are stored privately and served only to members of your workspace. We don't discuss your numbers or strategy with other clients or prospects. We only use your name, words or figures publicly with a specific permission you record, and you can withdraw it.

**Something is broken or stuck. What do I do?**
Go to **Help → Send to Threadline**. Tick **This is stopping us from doing something** if it's blocking you.

**How do I leave?**
Tell us. Offboarding prepares a full export, disconnects any accounts and stops all queued work. See [Offboarding and export](OFFBOARDING_AND_EXPORT.md).
