# Results and attribution: what counts, and how we know

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing. Sources: Master Blueprint and SOP Router (attribution classes), Living SOP Engine (EXPECTED vs ACTUAL, four-week report).

## What we measure, separately

1. **Operational:** what shipped, on time, with how many revisions.
2. **Attention:** who saw it and stayed, and whether they were the right people.
3. **Commercial intent:** profile visits, replies, enquiries, booked calls.
4. **Commercial outcome:** proposals and work won.

Views are an observation, not an outcome. We don't promise views, leads or revenue.

## How strongly each result is evidenced

| Class | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Directly tracked** | A tracked link, form or booking source ties it to a piece | Booked through the link in a post |
| **Buyer-named, client-attributed** | The buyer said which piece or channel brought them | "I saw your post about governance" |
| **Multi-touch influenced** | The buyer saw several pieces on the way | Followed for weeks, then enquired |
| **Associated / correlated** | It moved at the same time; no direct link | Enquiries rose the month output rose |
| **Qualitative only** | A comment or impression, not a count | A client mentions your content on a call |

Every figure in your reports carries its class. We don't claim a cause the evidence doesn't support.

## Expected versus actual

Before a piece goes out, Threadline records what it expects (and why). After it's out, we compare:

**expected → actual → why → change → retest.**

That comparison, not the raw numbers, is what makes each cycle better. Scores are decision aids against a stated rubric, not objective truth.

## What we need from you each week

Threadline can't see your inbox, calls or CRM. Once a week, tell us (**Commercial signal** on the **Performance** page, or on a call):
- new enquiries and where they said they came from;
- calls booked and held;
- proposals sent and work won or lost;
- anything a buyer said about your content.

A "none this week" answer is useful too.
