# Review and approval

> Status: drafted; owner review pending. Client-facing. Page: **Approvals**.

Nothing goes out without your approval.

## The queue

**Approvals** lists every script, edit and packaging waiting on you, oldest first. Each item shows:

- its type (Script, Edit or Packaging);
- the version you're approving;
- how long it's been waiting.

Items waiting three days or more are highlighted, and your backup contact hears about them.

## Approving one item

Open the item, then either:

- **approve it**, or
- **Request changes**, with a note saying what to change. A request for changes without a note is refused.

## Approving several at once

1. Tick the items, or use **Select all you can approve**.
2. Press **Approve selected**.
3. Confirm "Approve N items exactly as you see them now?"

Anything that changed after you opened the page is **skipped**, not approved, and left for you to look at again. The message says how many were approved, skipped or refused.

Only approvals can be done in bulk. Asking for changes needs a note on each item.

## Versions

- An approval covers **that exact version**. If a script, cut or packaging changes afterwards, it comes back for approval and can't be published until it's approved again.
- Comments show which cut they were about. Feedback left on an earlier cut is labelled **Earlier version**.

## What blocks approval

Packaging can't be approved if:

- it contains a figure the script doesn't contain;
- it contains an unverified figure; or
- it contains promise language, such as a guaranteed result.

The message says what to fix. This protects you from claims you can't stand behind.

## Who can approve

Admins, and members with the **Approver** profile. Other people see the queue but can't approve.
