# Your first four-week period: Establish / Calibrate

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing. Sources: Master Blueprint §9 (Day-7 installation), verbatim library J (12-week model), Living SOP Engine (four-week report). No dates or response times are promised here; your workspace shows the real ones.

## Week 1: installation

- Onboarding starts as soon as the agreement is signed, with no dead time.
- Pre-kickoff list sent; kickoff held (60–90 minutes).
- Brand Brain prefilled by Threadline and confirmed by you.
- First market-intelligence cycle and a primary constraint hypothesis.
- **3–5 core messages or pillars** agreed and your strategy for the period approved.
- **The first 6–8 useful scripts** written.
- Recording setup reviewed (if video is prescribed) and the first recording made.
- The aim is to finish installation by Day 7. It's an aim, not a guarantee; the **Installation** page shows each step's owner and anything blocking it.

## Weeks 2–4: first pieces into the market

- Record, review and approve in batches; published through connected accounts or by hand.
- Before each piece goes out, Threadline records what it expects the piece to do.
- A weekly report: what shipped, what we expected, what happened, what we're testing next.
- You send your weekly commercial signals (enquiries, calls, deals Threadline can't see).

## End of the period: the four-week review

Action → Results → Problems → Future, answering where we started, what was weak, what we changed, what happened, what we learned, the current weakest link, the next intervention, our confidence and the next test.

**How to read the first period:** the first pieces aren't meant to represent the finished system. They give the first real behavioural data from your market. A weak week is reported as a weak week.

## After that

- **Period 2, Refine / Correct:** diagnose what the market responded to; improve hooks, topics, delivery and packaging.
- **Period 3, Compound / Concentrate:** push harder into what the evidence supports.
- **Period 4+, Compound harder:** if you continue, a richer history and learning loop.
