# Welcome to Threadline

> Status: drafted; owner review pending. Client-facing. Reflects the app as built on 27 September 2026.

Threadline runs a managed authority system around your expertise. You do four things: **talk, record, approve and sell**. Threadline runs the rest: research, strategy, scripts, editing, packaging, scheduling, measurement and learning.

## What happens after you sign

| Step | What happens | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Agreement and first payment | We confirm the agreed scope, the approver and the payment schedule. Full delivery starts once the agreement is signed and the payment condition is met. | You and Threadline |
| 2. Your invitation | You get an email with a link. You choose your own password and land in your workspace. If the email doesn't arrive, we send you the link directly. | Threadline |
| 3. Onboarding | You fill in the onboarding in your workspace: your business, your voice and your operation. It saves as you go, so you can stop and come back. | You |
| 4. Kickoff | A conversation that captures your beliefs, stories and voice, and agrees your **early win** in words. | Together |
| 5. Installation | We work through the steps on the **Installation** page: business context captured, then constraint diagnosis, the first intelligence brief, your strategy approved, the first researched scripts, the recording setup checked, the first recording, and the first assets in production. We aim to finish within the first week. The page shows every step's owner and status, and anything blocking it. | Shared |
| 6. Sign-off | When every step is done, a workspace admin clicks **Sign off installation**. | You |
| 7. First four-week period | Recording, approvals, publishing and a weekly report. At the end of the period there's a four-week review. | Shared |

The first engagement is twelve weeks, in three four-week periods:

1. **Establish / calibrate:** get into the market consistently and set a baseline.
2. **Refine / correct:** diagnose what the market responded to, then change it.
3. **Concentrate / compound:** push harder on what the evidence supports.

Early pieces are data, not a verdict.

## Who does what

| You (client) | Threadline |
| --- | --- |
| Talk: onboarding, kickoff, source material | Research and market intelligence |
| Record the approved scripts in the **Recording Room** | Strategy, ideas and scripts |
| Approve, or ask for changes, in **Approvals** | Editing, quality checks and packaging |
| Sell: take the conversations the content starts, and tell us about them | Scheduling and publishing (through connected accounts or by hand) |
| Name one approver and keep approvals moving | Measurement, diagnosis, weekly reports and four-week reviews |

We don't promise views, leads, revenue or a set number of hours of your time. The time you spend is recorded on **Your time**, so it's measured rather than assumed.

## Where things are

- **This week**: what needs you now.
- **Recording**: the **Recording Room** and **Your setup**.
- **Approvals**: everything waiting on your decision.
- **Content**: what's in production, what's scheduled, and the **Library**.
- **Intelligence**: briefs, the diagnosis and the **Brand Brain**.
- **Results**: what we're learning, plus the overview, proof and pipeline.
- **Reports**
- **Settings**: your workspace, people, access and billing.
- **Help**: ask Threadline anything.
