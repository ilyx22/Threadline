# Welcome to Threadline

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing. Reflects the app as built on 26 September 2026.

Threadline runs a managed founder-authority and qualified-demand system around your expertise. You do four things: **talk, record, approve and sell**. Threadline runs the rest: research, strategy, scripts, editing, packaging, scheduling, measurement and learning.

## What happens after you sign

| Step | What happens | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Agreement and first payment | We confirm the agreed scope, the approver and the payment schedule. Full delivery starts once the agreement is signed and the payment condition is met. | You and Threadline |
| 2. Your invitation | You get an email with a link. You choose your own password and land in your workspace. If the email doesn't arrive, we send you the link directly. | Threadline |
| 3. Before the kickoff (about 10 minutes) | Starts straight after signature, with no dead time. In your workspace you fill in two short sections, your business basics and what we can access, and send what you already have from the short [pre-kickoff checklist](PRE_KICKOFF_CHECKLIST.md). That's all. Everything else is done with you on the call. | You |
| 4. Kickoff (60–90 minutes) | The Brand Brain session: your offer, customers, beliefs, stories and voice, your editing style and visual references, and your **early win** agreed in words. You talk; Threadline fills in the rest of your onboarding while we do. See the [kickoff agenda](KICKOFF_AGENDA.md). | Together |
| 4a. Check and confirm (about 10 minutes) | After the call you get a notice to check what we captured. Answers we typed are marked **From your call**. Correct anything we got wrong, then confirm, and your workspace is built from it. | You |
| 5. Installation | We work through the steps on the **Installation** page: business context captured, then constraint diagnosis, the first intelligence brief, your strategy approved, the first researched scripts, the recording setup checked, the first recording, and the first assets in production. The aim is to finish within the first week (Day 7), with 3–5 core messages or pillars agreed and the first 6–8 useful scripts ready; it is an aim, not a guarantee. The page shows every step's owner and status, and anything blocking it. | Shared |
| 6. Sign-off | When every step is done, a workspace admin clicks **Sign off installation**. | You |
| 7. First four-week period | Recording, approvals, publishing and a weekly report. At the end of the period there's a four-week review. | Shared |

The first engagement is twelve weeks, in three four-week periods:

1. **Period 1, Establish / Calibrate:** get into the market consistently and set a baseline.
2. **Period 2, Refine / Correct:** diagnose what the market responded to, then change it.
3. **Period 3, Compound / Concentrate:** push harder on what the evidence supports.
4. **Period 4+, Compound harder** (if you continue): more history, a larger library and a richer learning loop.

See the [first-period roadmap](FIRST_PERIOD_ROADMAP.md).

Early pieces are data, not a verdict.

## Who does what

| You (client) | Threadline |
| --- | --- |
| Talk: onboarding, kickoff, source material | Research and market intelligence |
| Record the approved scripts in the **Recording Room** | Strategy, ideas and scripts |
| Approve, or ask for changes, in **Approvals** | Editing, quality checks and packaging |
| Sell: take the conversations the content starts, and tell us about them | Scheduling and publishing (through connected accounts or by hand) |
| Name one approver and keep approvals moving | Measurement, diagnosis, weekly reports and four-week reviews |

We don't promise views, leads, revenue or a set number of hours of your time. The time you spend is recorded on **Your time**, so it's measured rather than assumed.

Two more things only you can do:
- **Commercial signals, weekly:** tell us about the enquiries, calls and deals Threadline can't see (**Commercial signal** on the **Performance** page). See the [results and attribution guide](RESULTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION_GUIDE.md).
- **Comments and DMs stay human:** you, or someone you name, reply to people. Threadline doesn't run automated engagement.

## What is and isn't included

- **Included:** the agreed core assets for each four-week period across up to about three agreed channels, the research, scripting, editing, packaging, publishing, measurement and reviews around them. The platform mix is prescribed after diagnosis; delivery can be video-led or text-led.
- **Not included** unless separately agreed: paid ads, funnels, email marketing, appointment setting, web development, daily community management and unlimited revisions. A long-form YouTube series is a pilot or custom item. Out-of-scope requests are logged (they tell us what clients need), not silently absorbed.
- **Editing standard:** we show examples of standard Threadline editing before or at onboarding. Heavier creator-style editing is scoped and priced separately.
- **Ownership:** you own the agreed outputs; Threadline keeps its background methods and tools. *(Wording subject to legal review.)*
- **Access:** we only ask for access through each platform's own delegation or connection. Never send us a password.
- **Publishing:** through each platform's official route where connected; posting by hand is the fallback.

## Proof, only if you want to

“One thing I ask every client at the start: if at the end of the initial 12 weeks you feel we’ve genuinely created something worth talking about, would you be open to a short recorded success interview? Completely optional, and obviously only if you’re happy with the result.”

We record your answer as YES, MAYBE or NO, in your own words. It isn't permission to publish anything: any public use is asked for separately, and a NO is respected.

## Where things are

- **This week**: what needs you now.
- **Recording**: the **Recording Room** and **Your setup**.
- **Approvals**: everything waiting on your decision.
- **Content**: what's in production, what's scheduled, and the **Library**.
- **Intelligence**: briefs, the diagnosis and the **Brand Brain**.
- **Results**: what we're learning, plus the overview, proof and pipeline.
- **Reports**
- **Settings**: your workspace, people, access and billing.
- **Help**: ask Threadline anything.
