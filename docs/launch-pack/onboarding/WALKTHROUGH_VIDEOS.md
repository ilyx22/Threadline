# Walkthrough videos (C07)

> **Status:** DRAFT for owner review (3 Nov 2026). Nothing has been recorded yet.

## What these are

Short, evergreen screen recordings, one per onboarding section. They're optional: every guide works without them.

**Where the links go**
- They sit as a single line at the end of the matching guide section: "Prefer to watch? 2-minute walkthrough".
- They also go on the workspace **Help** page.
- **They are never a required step**, and never the only way to find something out.
- The app has no video slot. These are links in the guides and Help, not a new feature.

**Who records them, and how**
- The owner records them with Loom (free plan) or any screen recorder.
- **Screen only, with no face and no name.** Narration says "we" and "Threadline", or there's no narration and captions are burned in. This keeps the content company-led under the outside-business rule.
- Use the labelled test workspace (`test-dry-run`), never a client's.

**Rules for every video**
- **2 minutes maximum.** If it runs longer, split it.
- Show the real screen as it is today. If a screen changes, re-record that one video.
- **No time promises beyond what's in the guides.** The only figures allowed are the ones we've already stated: about 10 minutes before kickoff, the 60–90 minute kickoff, about 10 minutes to check and confirm, and about 2 minutes for pre-flight.
- No results, no proof and no guarantees.
- The "Synthetic — not a client" banner must stay visible, or be cropped out completely. Never show synthetic data as if it were a client's.
- Captions on, because most people watch muted.

## A script outline for each (spoken or captions)

Each one is about 6 short lines, one per screen. Example, V2:

1. "Before your kickoff, there are two short sections, about ten minutes."
2. "Business basics: what you sell and who to. Short answers are fine."
3. "Access: invite anyone who'll help. We never ask for your passwords."
4. "It saves as you go, so you can stop and come back."
5. "When both are done, you'll see 'ready for the call'."
6. "On the call we fill in the rest while you talk."

Write the other five the same way, straight from the guide each one sits under. Don't add any claim that isn't already in that guide.
