# Brand Brain

> Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not approved). Client-facing. The page is **Intelligence → Brand Brain**.

The Brand Brain is what Threadline writes from. It holds:

- your company, offer and audiences;
- your founder story, beliefs and experience;
- your voice ("sounds like me" and "does NOT sound like me");
- proof you're cleared to state;
- content pillars and calls to action;
- content rules: banned topics, never-use phrases and compliance notes.

Banned topics and never-use phrases are passed to every draft as rules, then checked again before approval: a script or post that names one can't be approved until a person changes it. The check matches the words you gave us, so a person still reviews every piece for anything said another way.

## How it gets filled

- **Onboarding:** your answers go straight into the Brand Brain. Rather than repeat the onboarding questions here, open the onboarding in your workspace.
- **Threadline prefill:** we may draft sections from your calls and material. Those sections are listed under **Filled in by Threadline, waiting for your confirmation**. Check each one, then either press **Confirm** or edit it; an edit counts as confirmation.
- **Proof:** anything entered as proof (results, testimonials, credentials) is marked for review before it's stated as fact.
- **Changes later:** tell us through **Help**, or, as an admin, edit the section yourself.

## Who can change it

A workspace **Admin** can edit it. Members can read it.

## Versions

- Every change is kept as a version, and nothing is deleted. **Restore** brings back an earlier version as a new version.
- Drafts written against an older version are listed ("Drafts written against an older version") so they can be checked or generated again.

## Installation sign-off

The **Installation** page tracks the first-week steps: business context captured, then constraint diagnosis, the first intelligence brief, the strategy approved, the first researched scripts, the recording setup checked, the first recording, and the first assets in production. Each step shows its owner (you, Threadline, or together), its status, and any blocker.

- **Early win:** at kickoff, Threadline records an **Early win** in words (**Agree it**). When it happens, **Record it happened** notes the date and what shows it happened.
- **Sign off installation:** available to a workspace admin once every step is complete. Until then it stays closed, showing "Sign-off opens once every step above is complete".
