# Live call: one page

**Status:** drafted; owner approval pending. Internal only. A print version is in `LIVE_CALL_ONE_PAGER.html` (A4, one page).
**Call:** 45 minutes. It ends in one state: WON / FOLLOW_UP / PROPOSAL_PROCESS / NOT_FIT / LOST, each with a dated next action.
**[V-X]** means read passage X word for word from `APPROVED_VERBATIM_LIBRARY.md`.

| # | Stage | Min | Ask or do | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **OPEN** | 3 | Agenda. "Why now?" "Who else decides?" | |
| 2 | **ECONOMICS** | 6 | Deal value · margin · lifetime · cycle · close rate · capacity · value of one extra qualified opportunity · current sources | Log on the prospect |
| 3 | **CURRENT** | 6 | How clients find you · who researches, writes, records, edits, approves and publishes · what stalls · your time (their answer) | Arrive knowing, then verify |
| 4 | **DESIRED** | 3 | "If content worked 90 days from now, what's different?" · "What would prove it commercially?" | Their words |
| 5 | **CONSTRAINT** | 4 | Topics · positioning · scripting · recording · QA · approvals · distribution · conversion path · learning · coordination | 1–3 hypotheses |
| 6 | **CONSEQUENCE** | 3 | Cost of no change: time, consistency, pipeline, pricing power | Reframe in their words, then get agreement |
| 7 | **PRESCRIPTION** | 3 | The standard 80% process plus their configured 20%. You talk, record, approve and sell; Threadline runs the rest. | [V-I] |
| 8 | **DEMO** | 5 | One path matched to the constraint; synthetic workspace only | [V-H] |
| 9 | **COMMERCIALS** | 7 | £2,500 implementation + £2,500 **every four weeks**; initial 12 weeks = 3 periods = **£10,000**. One offer. No guarantees. | [V-D] [V-E] [V-G] [V-J] |
| 10 | **DECISION** | 5 | Ask plainly. No fake urgency. WON → SOP 04 the same day; ask [V-B] | [V-B] |

**Objections.** Proof → [V-A] (the non-founder adaptation is in the guide) · AI → Vault "AI is internal leverage…" · Time → no hours figure; "measured, not assumed" · Price → [V-D] with their own numbers, then compare against the real alternative · Alternatives → Vault "compare the full…" · Speed → [V-C], [V-H] · One month → [V-G] · Guarantee → Vault "Not responsibly…"

**Never say:**
- "monthly"
- "nothing to lose"
- "0.25 clients"
- "one client in 90 days"
- a follower target
- "the algorithm needs 90 days"
- a number of founder hours
- anything that implies case studies exist privately

**Headline if it fits:** “We are not trying to make you famous. We are trying to make you familiar to the people who matter.” [V-F]

**Afterwards (within 24 hours):**
- Log the state, exact objections, value maths, scope requests, and the next action with its date.
- Send the recap in their own words.
- Send nothing you didn't promise.
