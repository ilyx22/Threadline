**Trigger:** the prospect says yes (end state WON). Everything below happens the same day where possible.

## Same-day actions (SOP 04)

1. Confirm the legal company, the client contact and billing details.
2. Send the agreement, order form or SOW with **only the agreed scope**.
3. Send payment, invoice or checkout instructions.
4. Send a short welcome note explaining what happens after signature and payment.
5. Create the client workspace only once the deal is real enough to justify it.
6. Book the **60–90 minute Brand Brain / kickoff** session.
7. Send the minimal pre-kickoff request:
   - website and offer links;
   - existing content channels;
   - relevant proof;
   - the access method (delegated or native access only; never ask for passwords);
   - sales-call or customer sources, if available.
8. Confirm the approval SLA and who is authorised to approve.
9. Confirm who owns recording, and the expected cadence.
10. Record the commercial baseline start date.

## Confirm the terms out loud (SOP 04)

Confirm £2,500 implementation + £2,500 every 4 weeks, a 12-week initial engagement / 3 service periods (£10,000 initial TCV), the agreed scope and dependencies, and the actual payment schedule.

- Never say "monthly".
- Explain the arc: establish/calibrate → correct/refine → concentrate/compound.
- No guaranteed revenue, leads, views or fixed platform learning phase.

## Optional success-interview ask: VERBATIM (SOP 04 / library B)

> “One thing I ask every client at the start: if at the end of the initial 12 weeks you feel we’ve genuinely created something worth talking about, would you be open to a short recorded success interview? Completely optional, and obviously only if you’re happy with the result.”

- Record `testimonial_permission_if_successful` as YES / MAYBE / NO, with their exact wording.
- This is willingness to consider a later interview, **not** advance permission to publish. Seek separate approval for any public proof.
- Respect NO. Do not pressure clients whose results are weak.

- [ ] Agreement or order form accepted
- [ ] Payment condition satisfied
- [ ] Kickoff booked
- [ ] Workspace created
- [ ] Client owner and approver identified
- [ ] Essential source links received, or explicitly pending with a deadline

**If payment or signature stalls:** keep the person in sales follow-up. Do not begin full research or fulfilment on a vague promise.
