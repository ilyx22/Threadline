What is LinkedIn ghostwriting and how does it work for founders and executives? You sit for one deep voice interview (60-90 minutes) where we capture how you actually think, the contrarian opinions you hold, and the way you talk. From there, our writers produce 16 to 30 posts per month in your voice, with custom graphics included. You review, approve, we publish. You never have to open LinkedIn unless you want to engage with comments. Will the content actually sound like me, or like a generic ghostwriter? That's the entire point of voice calibration. We write 3-4 sample posts in distinctly different styles after the voice interview, you tell us which feels like you, and we refine until the content is indistinguishable from something you'd write yourself. We don't ship a single live post until you've signed off that the voice is right. How many LinkedIn posts do I get per month? 16 to 30 per month, plus custom graphics, carousels, and infographics where they make sense. The cadence is calibrated to LinkedIn's algorithm: four to seven posts per week, mixed across hot takes, educational content, personal stories, and product launches. We adjust based on what's performing in your specific feed. Do you handle posting, scheduling, and engagement, or do I post myself? We handle posting and scheduling end to end. You approve, we publish. Engagement (replying to comments, DMs from prospects, connection requests) is the part we coach you on rather than do for you, since the inbound conversations are where pipeline actually gets built and they need to come from you authentically. If you want that engagement worked harder, our Engagement Outreach add-on identifies which engagers fit your ICP and builds the outreach workflows for you, with messages drafted around the post they engaged with so scaling the follow-up doesn't mean sounding like a bot. How long until LinkedIn content actually drives pipeline? You'll see follower growth within your ICP and engagement lift in the first 30-60 days. Inbound DMs and meetings booked from LinkedIn typically start landing in months three to four. The real story is compounding. Month six tends to outperform month three by a wide margin, month twelve outperforms month six, and the audience you build keeps paying dividends for as long as you stay consistent. Every post that performs adds to your reach, every prospect who engages becomes a retargeting audience and an outbound trigger if you're running other Understory services, and the algorithm rewards the consistency more every month you keep at it.
