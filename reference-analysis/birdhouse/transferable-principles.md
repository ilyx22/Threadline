# Transferable principles — Birdhouse → Threadline

Each row: what was observed (measured where possible), why it works, the principle that transfers, how Threadline expresses it in its own world, the copy risk of the translation, and the implementation priority. The specimen is analysed; it is not the spec (see `forbidden-to-copy.md`).

| # | Observation | Why it works | Transferable principle | Threadline translation | Copy risk | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whole page sits on one soft tinted canvas (#DEF3FF) with white 40px-radius "paper" cards floating on it | The canvas reads as a place, not a template; cards feel physical | One warm canvas, paper surfaces on top, no grey section rules | Warm linen canvas (`--canvas`), cream paper cards with a 1.5px ink outline and a hard offset shadow — "printed card" rather than "floating card" | Low — colour, radius and shadow language all differ | P1 |
| 2 | Display serif at 90px/99px, regular weight, tight leading; body in a plain sans at 20/24 and 16/19 | Serif carries editorial authority; the sans keeps explanation plain | Editorial serif for statements, plain sans for explanation, big size gap between them | Fraunces (variable, optical sizes) for display at 72–88px desktop / 40–44px mobile; Inter for body; a mono for factory labels and stamps | Low — different family, different scale steps | P1 |
| 3 | Italic serif eyebrow ("Founders, CEOs, Business Owners:") names the audience before the headline | The reader self-selects in one second | Name the buyer before the promise | Mono-caps eyebrow stamp "FOR EXPERT-LED BUSINESSES" on a tag shape, not italic serif | Low | P1 |
| 4 | Platform names replaced by icon chips inline in the headline | Turns a list into a picture | Let a picture replace a list where the reader already knows the picture | Inline "package label" chips in the branching section (one thesis → many packages); never inline in the H1 | Medium if chips were circular cyan — Threadline uses square labels with a stroke | P2 |
| 5 | Hero = statement left, illustration cluster right, illustration overlapping the card edge | Composition has depth; the eye moves headline → picture → CTA | Overlap and asymmetry make a page feel authored | Hero: statement left, the Threadline machine right with the conveyor running off the card's edge; the founder's raw crates sit on the canvas outside the card | Low — original subject and geometry | P1 |
| 6 | Illustrated edges (grass, clouds) instead of hard section boundaries | Sections feel like scenes | Transitions can be part of the illustration system | Conveyor belts and thread lines carry the eye from one section to the next; a section's bottom edge is where the thread exits | Low | P2 |
| 7 | Anthropomorphised mascots hold props (phone, cash, laptop) beside each claim | A character makes a number memorable | Give abstract claims a physical actor | Original cast: Founder, Operator, Scanner, Inspector, Builder, Buyer, Signal pulse — humans and machines, not creatures, drawn as 2px ink line figures with flat fills | Medium — the risk is any bird, house or tree; none appear | P1 |
| 8 | Newsletter: every post is one physical scene (claw machine, hose over pots, folding chairs) whose headline explains the scene | Physical systems are understood and remembered | Turn abstract principles into simple physical processes | The Authority Factory: raw expertise enters, machines transform it, packages exit, market response returns along a pipe, the next batch is better informed | Low — different metaphor family | P1 |
| 9 | Large numbers in a 2×2 stat cluster with characters | Numbers plus faces read as proof | Proof needs to be legible at a glance | Threadline has no client proof yet, so the cluster is *product proof*: a real (synthetic-labelled) expectation → actual → diagnosis chain, not counts | Medium — the layout shape is generic; the content is the difference | P1 |
| 10 | Cyan pill CTAs (60px radius), same colour everywhere, white text, no shadow | One CTA colour = one action | One action colour, used only for the action | Ember (`--accent`) rectangular buttons with 12px radius and a hard offset shadow that "presses" on click; one colour for Apply only | Low | P1 |
| 11 | Motion is light: hover 0.3s, one marquee, carousels | Illustration does the work, not choreography | Do not animate everything; animate what teaches | Threadline motion has a job list: conveyor (teaches flow), thread draw (teaches lineage), stamp (teaches gate), pipe return (teaches learning); each has a reduced-motion still | Low | P1 |
| 12 | Nav: four items + APPLY; header not sticky; mobile keeps only logo + APPLY | Fewer choices, one action | Small nav, one visible action at every width | Threadline nav: How it works · Who it is for · Playbook · Apply (+ Sign in); sticky on scroll because the page is long and narrative | Low | P1 |
| 13 | Value-first channel on a separate domain (newsletter, gated playbook) with the agency site selling directly | Free value earns trust before the ask | Give away something unusually valuable, then ask | `/playbook`: The Founder Authority System — an interactive illustrated resource on the main domain, gated by nothing on the first chapters, email optional later (not built to gate at launch) | Medium if the resource were called a "Playbook" with the same chapter names — Threadline uses its own chapters | P2 |
| 14 | Social proof leads (logo strip, stats, videos) | Reduces perceived risk | Show proof early | Threadline cannot show client proof honestly yet; it shows the mechanism and says so — no invented logos, numbers or testimonials | High if copied literally; Threadline's claims ledger forbids it | P1 (as a refusal) |
| 15 | FAQ answers commit to specifics ("10–14 days", "from $5,000/month", "results from Day one") | Specificity converts | Be specific where you can be truthful | Specifics Threadline can state: £2,500 implementation + £2,500 every 4 weeks, 12-week initial engagement, three periods; no results-timing claim | Low | P1 |
| 16 | Full-bleed wordmark in the footer | Ends with identity | The last frame should be the brand | Footer: THREADLINE set large in the display serif on the canvas with the thread running through it | Low | P2 |
| 17 | Responsive: h1 90 → 64 → 48px; hero stacks headline over illustration at 390; nav collapses to logo + CTA | Story survives the narrow width | Mobile keeps the story, simplifies the machinery | At 390 the factory becomes a single vertical conveyor; characters reduce to one per station; motion reduces to the belt | Low | P1 |
| 18 | Document heights 8k–14k px | Long pages are fine when each screen is one idea | One idea per screen | Homepage sections each answer one question in the buyer's order | Low | P1 |
