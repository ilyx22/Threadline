# DRAFT - BRANDING PENDING
# SOP 00 - Market Validation + Niche Gate (V14 Course-Authority)

## Authority
The governing principle is the reviewed course stack: niche selection -> total immersion -> problem/solution validation -> offer -> acquisition. The mentor transcript is used only as a secondary implementation example where it agrees with that stack.

## Trigger
Run before scaled outbound, before treating a niche/problem as "locked", and whenever a niche change is being considered.

## Objective
Select one active niche and one expensive problem using economics, immersion and direct buyer evidence rather than founder taste.

## Phase A - candidate wedge selection
1. Define 2-3 candidate wedges inside the agreed expert-led B2B category.
2. Score each on: money/economics, painful recurring problem, reachability, founder/expert trust importance, proven offer prevalence, capacity for more demand, content opportunity, and ability to identify decision-makers.
3. Consider founder unfair advantages only as a tie-breaker or access advantage; do not mistake personal familiarity for market proof.
4. Pick ONE wedge for the immersion sprint.

## Phase B - total immersion
5. Review 25-50 companies and at least 20 founder/content examples in the selected wedge.
6. Capture at least 20 pieces of market language: pains, desires, objections, triggers, alternatives, current workflows, buying language, maturity-stage differences.
7. Document what they already buy, how content is currently produced, where content breaks, who owns it, what a customer is worth, and where the buyer is reliably reachable.
8. Create a problem bank of at least 10 observable symptoms and 3-5 candidate root problems. Every problem can later become an outreach angle or content hook; do not confuse a symptom with the core problem.
9. Define A/B/C-fit rules and hard disqualifiers.

## Phase C - direct problem/solution validation
10. Book **five research conversations** with qualified people from the wedge. These are not disguised sales calls.
11. Open with the truth: Threadline is building a defined service for this market and wants to understand how they currently handle founder-led content and demand creation. Offer a useful diagnosis/feedback in return for their time.
12. Ask neutral questions before presenting Threadline's hypothesis:
   - Walk me through how you currently create founder/expert content.
   - Who researches, chooses topics, writes, records, edits, approves and publishes?
   - What is the most annoying or expensive part?
   - What repeatedly stalls?
   - What have you tried to fix it?
   - What happened?
   - What would make content commercially useful over the next 90 days?
   - Where would better content matter commercially: authority, inbound conversations, sales enablement, pipeline, time released?
   - If the core problem disappeared, what would materially change?
13. Record exact language and quantify where possible. Do not lead them into saying Threadline's preferred answer.
14. Use the mentor's **4-of-5 same-core-pain** rule only as a practical validation heuristic. The course principle - real problem/solution validation - is authoritative. If evidence is mixed, do not force a pass; run more interviews or change the hypothesis.
15. A "same pain" means the same underlying expensive problem, not identical words.

## Phase D - freeze the first commercial hypothesis
16. Write one sentence for each:
   - **Market:** exactly who we are serving first.
   - **Qualification:** what must already be true for them to buy/use Threadline successfully.
   - **Core problem:** the expensive recurring problem the market itself confirmed.
   - **Immediate result:** high-quality founder content people want to watch.
   - **Commercial result:** authority and qualified demand/pipeline.
   - **Secondary result:** less founder coordination burden.
   - **Mechanism:** the Threadline evidence -> strategy -> creation -> production -> distribution -> learning loop.
17. Confirm the first prospect list can be sourced and reached without inventing a channel preference.
18. Freeze this niche/problem hypothesis for the first controlled sales sample. Do not reopen it because a few messages feel uncomfortable.

## Definition of done
All are true:
- one wedge selected;
- immersion brief complete;
- qualification/disqualifiers defined;
- five buyer conversations completed (or a documented reason a slightly larger sample was needed);
- market language captured;
- a recurring expensive core pain is validated strongly enough to test commercially;
- one market + one problem + one outcome hypothesis is written;
- reachability and lead source are proven;
- first 100 A/B-fit prospects can be built.

If the validation signal is weak, LOOP inside SOP 00. Do not solve uncertainty by building more software.
