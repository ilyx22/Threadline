\# SOP 08 \- Proof Capture \+ Attribution

\#\# Trigger  
Any meaningful result, testimonial-like statement, commercial signal or operational improvement worth preserving.

\#\# Objective  
Capture proof while it is fresh, with enough evidence/context that it can later become a defensible case study. Threadline should prove value across operational efficiency, attention, commercial intent and commercial outcomes without pretending organic attribution is as deterministic as paid ads.

\#\# Day-0 baseline prerequisite  
For every new client, preserve a comparable pre-Threadline window where possible (normally prior 30–90 days):  
\- founder hours spent on content;  
\- core assets/month and consistency;  
\- production cycle time, approval time and revision burden;  
\- views, watch time/retention, saves, shares, profile visits and audience quality where available;  
\- site/profile/link traffic;  
\- inbound DMs/enquiries;  
\- booked calls;  
\- qualified opportunities/pipeline;  
\- closed-won revenue and known lead source.  
Label each field MEASURED / CLIENT-REPORTED / UNAVAILABLE.

\#\# Per-asset tracking  
Every published asset should have:  
1\. Persistent content/asset ID.  
2\. Client, platform/channel, publish date and live URL.  
3\. Pillar/theme/angle.  
4\. CTA and destination.  
5\. Raw destination URL and tracked/UTM URL where applicable.  
6\. Native performance metrics on the agreed cadence.  
7\. Linked commercial signals/events when identifiable.

\#\# Minimum tracking stack  
Do not wait for a complex integration. Use:  
\- asset/content ID \+ live URL ledger;  
\- tracked links/UTMs for owned destinations where possible;  
\- external CRM source fields plus opportunity/deal value;  
\- booking/application source question such as “How did you hear about us / what content did you see?”;  
\- weekly Commercial Signal Capture from the client for DMs, calls, opportunities and sales Threadline cannot observe;  
\- platform-native analytics.  
An attribution provider such as Trakyo or equivalent is optional future infrastructure and should pass SOP 11 before becoming a dependency.

\#\# Four proof layers  
\#\#\# 1\. Operational ROI  
Founder hours, output, cycle time, approval time, revision burden and coordination load.

\#\#\# 2\. Attention  
Views, retention/watch time, saves, shares, comments, profile visits and audience quality. These are leading indicators, not ROI by themselves.

\#\#\# 3\. Commercial intent  
Tracked clicks, opt-ins, DMs, enquiries and booked calls.

\#\#\# 4\. Commercial outcome  
Qualified opportunities, pipeline value, closed-won deals/revenue and buyer/sales notes where observable.

\#\# Capture immediately when a result appears  
1\. Date/time window.  
2\. Client \+ baseline state.  
3\. Exact result/event.  
4\. Evidence source: platform analytics, CRM, booking system, client message, buyer statement, sales-call note, tracking link, timing ledger, etc.  
5\. Threadline activity/content asset(s) preceding it.  
6\. Attribution strength.  
7\. Screenshot/file/live URL/content ID where appropriate.  
8\. Permission state: internal only / anonymised / client-approved public.  
9\. Whether figure is measured or client-reported.

\#\# Attribution classes  
Use one of:  
\- DIRECTLY\_TRACKED — tracked link/content ID/booking/CRM path supports the connection;  
\- BUYER\_NAMED\_CLIENT\_ATTRIBUTED — buyer or client explicitly says content influenced the action;  
\- MULTI\_TOUCH\_INFLUENCED — content is one identifiable touch in a broader journey;  
\- ASSOCIATED\_CORRELATED — movement occurred during the period but causality is not established;  
\- QUALITATIVE\_ONLY — authority, trust or buyer language without a defensible monetary path.  
Preserve first-touch and last-touch separately where supported. Never upgrade influence/correlation into a causal claim without evidence.

\#\# Useful normalized metrics once data quality is sufficient  
\- booked calls per 10,000 views;  
\- qualified opportunities per 10,000 views;  
\- qualified pipeline per 10,000 views;  
\- revenue per asset;  
\- revenue per 1,000 views;  
\- commercial value by topic/pillar/CTA.  
Do not calculate/present monetary efficiency when attribution coverage is weak.

\#\# Case-study threshold  
Create a case study only when there is a coherent before \-\> intervention \-\> after story with sufficient evidence and client permission. Do not manufacture causality from one viral post.

\#\# Client Proof Interview  
After a meaningful win:  
Before: prepare baseline, original constraint, Threadline intervention and measured changes.  
During: who are you \-\> before state \-\> why hire \-\> how process helped \-\> results/milestones \-\> unexpected benefits \-\> advice \-\> what would you tell another prospect.  
After: full case study, testimonial clips, short-form proof, written proof, website block, proposal proof, sales-call evidence and founder-content asset.

\#\# Testimonial ask  
Ask only after real value is visible. Make it easy: ask what changed, what they valued, and what surprised them; do not script a fake quote for them.

\#\# Definition of done  
Evidence is stored, asset/source path is linked where possible, attribution language chosen, measured-vs-reported status known, permission state known, and any public use is traceable to approval.
