# SOP 07 - Monthly Strategy + Retention

## Trigger
Monthly strategy/reporting checkpoint or renewal/retention conversation.

## Objective
Prove Threadline is learning and making commercially intelligent decisions, not merely shipping posts.

## Prepare before call/report
- shipped assets/output;
- performance trends and outliers;
- qualified comments/inquiries/calls/opportunities where trackable;
- founder time/cycle-time changes;
- current constraint vs previous month;
- tests run and what was learned;
- client blockers/dependencies;
- next recommended experiments.

## Client-facing structure
### 1. ACTION
What Threadline actually did.
### 2. RESULTS
What changed. Distinguish views/engagement from commercial signals. Use careful attribution language.
### 3. PROBLEMS
What currently constrains growth/production/conversion. Include client-side blockers without blame.
### 4. FUTURE
What we will test/do next month and what we need from the client.

## Retention health check
Ask/assess:
- Is the client seeing strategic understanding, not just output?
- Are we reducing founder coordination burden?
- Are approvals/recording becoming easier?
- Are the right audience/commercial signals improving?
- Is scope still economically sane for Threadline?

## Renewal logic
Do not wait until final week of term to discover dissatisfaction. If initial 3-month term is approaching end, discuss continuation once enough evidence exists to recommend the next phase.

## Definition of done
Next month's primary constraint/test plan agreed; unresolved blockers have owner/date; proof captured; client health state recorded.
