# SOP 04 - Close to Kickoff

## Trigger
Prospect says yes / agrees to proceed.

## Objective
Turn verbal intent into a properly activated client with clean expectations, payment, dates and responsibilities.

## Actions - same day where possible
1. Confirm legal company/client contact + billing details.
2. Send final agreement/order form/SOW with only agreed scope.
3. Send payment/invoice/checkout instructions.
4. Send concise welcome note explaining what happens after signature/payment.
5. Create client workspace only after the deal is real enough to justify it.
6. Book 60-90 minute Brand Brain/kickoff.
7. Send minimal pre-kickoff request: website, offer links, existing content channels, relevant proof, access method, sales-call/customer sources if available.
8. Confirm approval SLA and who is authorised to approve.
9. Confirm recording owner and expected cadence.
10. Record commercial baseline start date.

## Gate before fulfilment starts
- [ ] agreement/order form accepted;
- [ ] payment condition satisfied;
- [ ] kickoff booked;
- [ ] workspace created;
- [ ] client owner/approver identified;
- [ ] essential source links received or explicitly pending with deadline.

## Failure handling
If payment/signature stalls, keep the person in sales follow-up. Do not begin full research/fulfilment on a vague promise.

## NEXT SOP
When gate passes -> **SOP 05 - Client Installation / Day-7 Win**.

## Expectation and optional proof milestone
Confirm £2,500 implementation + £2,500 every 4 weeks, 12-week initial engagement / 3 service periods (£10,000 initial TCV), agreed scope/dependencies and actual payment schedule. Explain establish/calibrate -> correct/refine -> concentrate/compound. No guaranteed revenue, leads, views or fixed platform learning phase.

Preserve this optional ask verbatim:
“One thing I ask every client at the start: if at the end of the initial 12 weeks you feel we’ve genuinely created something worth talking about, would you be open to a short recorded success interview? Completely optional, and obviously only if you’re happy with the result.”

Record testimonial_permission_if_successful = YES / MAYBE / NO, with exact wording. This is willingness to consider a later interview, not advance permission to publish. Seek separate approval for actual public proof. Respect NO and do not pressure clients with weak results.
