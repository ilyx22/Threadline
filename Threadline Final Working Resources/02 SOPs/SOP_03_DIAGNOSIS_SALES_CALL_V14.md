# SOP 03 - Diagnosis Sales Call (V14 Productized-Service Sales)

## Authority
Course diagnosis/sales principles govern. The mentor's "arrive knowing" principle is retained only where it reinforces specialisation and productization; it must not become fake certainty.

## Trigger
A qualified prospect has a scheduled diagnosis/sales call after the active niche/problem hypothesis has passed SOP 00.

## Objective
Confirm whether the prospect fits the **defined Threadline productized service**, diagnose which part of the known problem is most acute, make the commercial case, and reach an explicit decision state without asking the buyer to design the service.

## 15-30 minutes before
- Review business, offer, founder, current content, recent signals and likely customer economics.
- Open the active niche brief and the validated problem bank.
- Write 1-3 hypotheses based on evidence. Label them hypotheses, not facts.
- Open CRM/application answers and the relevant 3-5 minute Threadline demo path.

## Call sequence
1. **Agenda** - understand their current system, test fit against the problem Threadline solves, show the relevant mechanism if fit, decide next step.
2. **Why now** - why did they take the call and what are they trying to improve?
3. **Confirm business economics** - primary offer, ideal buyer, typical deal/LTV where appropriate, acquisition channels, capacity, value of one good customer.
4. **Arrive knowing, then verify** - explain the recurring patterns Threadline sees in this niche. Ask which are true here. Never claim 95% certainty until evidence actually supports it.
5. **Narrow diagnosis** - investigate how the known problem manifests: research/topic selection, positioning, scripting, recording, editing/QA, approvals, distribution, conversion path, performance learning, founder coordination burden.
6. **Desired state** - define what commercially useful content means 90 days from now.
7. **Consequence/value** - quantify where possible: missed qualified demand, founder hours, slow output, wasted team/editor cost, weak authority, sales-enablement gap.
8. **Reframe** - summarise the problem in their words and confirm agreement.
9. **Prescribe the existing Threadline system** - do not ask "what would you like us to build?" Explain how the standard Threadline process addresses the diagnosed constraint and which 20% is configured to their business.
10. **Relevant demo only** - show evidence -> signal -> strategy -> idea/script -> record -> production -> publish -> performance/commercial signal -> learning. No 47-route tour.
11. **Offer** - present the one founding offer and division of labour. Do not create bespoke scope live merely because a prospect asks.
12. **Price + decision** - state terms clearly. One-call close is allowed when fit, trust and decision process support it; it is not a forced doctrine. No fake urgency.

## Productization rules during sales
- Threadline owns a defined 80% process; the client's business/voice/market/proof make up the configured 20%.
- If a request is outside the offer, classify it: `NOT_INCLUDED`, `POTENTIAL_ADD_ON_LATER`, or `REPEATED_DEMAND_TO_REVIEW`. Do not custom-build it to save one deal.
- Do not introduce three tiers at launch. The explicit Threadline decision is one offer while learning. Tiers/add-ons require repeated evidence.
- Do not discount because delivery is templated. Price is tied to outcome/economics, not hours worked.

## Immediately after
Log fit, exact language, problem severity, desired state, objections, offer reaction, value math where available, scope requests, next action/date and win/loss reason.

## Definition of done
Prospect is in exactly one state: NOT_FIT / FOLLOW_UP / PROPOSAL_PROCESS / WON, with a dated next action when applicable.

## NEXT SOP
WON -> SOP 04. Anything else -> SOP 02 and the dated follow-up.
