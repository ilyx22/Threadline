# Threadline YouTube Channel + Long-Form Playbook V2

## Strategic decision
YouTube is a high-potential channel for selected expert-led B2B clients because it can combine discovery with enough depth to establish trust before a sales conversation. It is not automatically the correct channel for every client and full long-form production is not silently bundled into the founding retainer.

## Qualification gate for long-form
Use YouTube long-form when most of these are true:
- prospects actively research the problem before buying;
- the founder has deep explanations, proof, opinions or frameworks;
- one customer has meaningful value so pre-sale trust matters;
- topics have useful shelf-life;
- the founder can record 10-30+ minutes of real substance;
- there is a clear path from viewer -> next step -> sales conversation;
- Threadline can produce it without destroying delivery economics.

If not, keep YouTube to Shorts or exclude it.

## Founding offer rule
- YouTube Shorts can count as one normal distribution channel.
- Long-form is `PILOT / CUSTOM` at launch.
- First pilot: 1 substantial long-form asset/month, either as an add-on or in exchange for a defined amount of short-form capacity.
- Time every task in the Delivery Load Ledger before setting a permanent price/scope.

## Operating loop
### 1. Market evidence
Start from a high-value problem, objection, decision, misconception, proof point or market change - not 'we need a video this week'.

### 2. Idea thesis
Define target viewer, promised value, commercial relevance, why this founder has credibility and what belief/action should change.

### 3. Package before script
Create 3-5 title directions and 2-3 thumbnail concepts before recording. If the value is not legible in title + thumbnail, the concept is not ready.

Current YouTube guidance puts major weight on accurate titles/thumbnails and notes that a very high share of top-performing videos use custom thumbnails. Threadline should therefore treat packaging as strategy, not post-production decoration.

### 4. Opening/hook
The first seconds must deliver on the title/thumbnail promise immediately. No 45-second corporate introduction. Establish why the viewer should stay and move into value.

### 5. Structure
For expertise-led B2B, default to:
- scripted opening;
- structured talking points / story beats;
- proof/examples;
- transitions that preserve a logical chain;
- concise CTA aligned to buying stage.
Do not force a teleprompter for 20 minutes if the founder is better with a point outline.

### 6. Recording
Native 16:9. Approved studio profile. Prefer clarity and conviction over cinematic excess.

### 7. Edit
Edit for comprehension and sustained interest. B-roll/graphics exist to explain, prove or reset attention - not to make every sentence twitch.

### 8. Publish and package test
Store title, thumbnail, description, chapters/links where useful and published URL. Where the platform/tools allow it, test packaging changes based on evidence rather than ego.

### 9. Multi-format extraction
Resolve the old-source/current-platform conflict this way:
- never promise that every long-form video yields many good Shorts;
- do extract moments that genuinely stand alone;
- YouTube currently supports turning public long-form videos into linked Shorts, which can aid discovery;
- when short-form is a major strategic channel, record native vertical versions/hooks rather than relying solely on crops.

### 10. Learn
Use a hierarchy:
1. **Audience fit / commercial relevance** - was this for the right buyer?
2. **Appeal** - impressions/CTR/context; did people choose it?
3. **Engagement** - retention/average view duration; did they stay?
4. **Satisfaction / returning audience** - did it create an audience relationship?
5. **Commercial signals** - relevant comments, DMs, inquiries, booked calls, sales-assist mentions and attributable links where trackable.

YouTube's current recommendation guidance explicitly treats Shorts, long-form, live and posts as formats the system can understand together, while warning that viewer preferences differ by format. So Threadline should test formats without assuming either that mixing formats 'confuses the algorithm' or that short viewers automatically become long-form viewers.

## Content jobs for an expert-led B2B channel
- **Problem recognition** - explain an expensive problem better than the market currently does.
- **Decision support** - help buyers choose approaches/vendors/processes.
- **Proof / teardown** - cases, audits, before/after logic, failures and lessons.
- **Point of view** - differentiated beliefs the market can associate with the founder.
- **Sales-assist library** - evergreen explanations salespeople can send before/after calls.

## Initial cadence
Do not force weekly long-form merely because creators say consistency matters. For the first 60 days, a stronger expert video every 2-4 weeks may be better than low-quality weekly output. Cadence is set by production economics and idea quality, then increased after the loop works.

## Pricing decision rule
Do not finalise the long-form add-on until the first pilot gives:
- research minutes;
- packaging minutes;
- scripting/outline minutes;
- founder recording time;
- edit/revision minutes;
- thumbnail minutes;
- upload/distribution minutes;
- analysis/reporting minutes;
- commercial value signal.
