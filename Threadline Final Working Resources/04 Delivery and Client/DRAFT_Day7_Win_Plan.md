# Threadline Day-7 Win Plan

## Objective
Create unmistakable progress before the client has had time to wonder whether they bought another content retainer.

## Day 1 - Understand
- Business/offer/ICP/voice captured.
- Existing content and commercial baseline captured.
- Required source access/links collected.

## Days 2-3 - Diagnose and research
- Initial Constraint Diagnosis drafted.
- Market/customer/competitor evidence collected with provenance.
- 3-5 meaningful findings promoted into candidate signals.

## Day 3 - Strategic direction
- Primary current constraint named.
- 3 messages/angles worth testing proposed.
- 30-day content hypothesis drafted.

## Day 4 - Recording-ready batch
- First 6-8 scripts prepared from approved evidence/signals.
- Claims checked.
- Recording queue sequenced by priority.

## Days 5-7 - Production begins
- Founder records.
- First assets enter editing/production.
- Client sees the complete evidence -> strategy -> execution chain.

## Client-visible Day-7 summary
- What we learned.
- What we believe the constraint is.
- What we are testing first.
- What is ready to record/produce.
- What we need from the client next.

## Blocker rule
If a client dependency blocks the milestone, record it explicitly. Do not silently convert a client blocker into a Threadline delivery failure or manufacture progress flags.
