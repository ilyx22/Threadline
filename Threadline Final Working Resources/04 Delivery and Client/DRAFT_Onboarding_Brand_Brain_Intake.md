# Threadline Onboarding + Brand Brain Intake

## Session goal
Capture enough context to understand the business, its customers, its founder voice and its commercial reality without turning onboarding into homework.

## Business and economics
- What do you sell? To whom? For how much?
- What makes a customer economically meaningful?
- Current revenue mix and acquisition sources.
- Capacity for additional demand.
- Sales process and typical cycle length.

## ICP and customer language
- Best customers and worst-fit customers.
- Trigger events that make someone buy.
- Top pains, desired outcomes, objections and misconceptions.
- Exact phrases customers use on calls, emails and reviews.
- What they have already tried before buying.

## Offer and positioning
- Primary offer and transformation.
- Proof available and proof that must not be used.
- Competitors/alternatives and why buyers choose you.
- Claims you can defend; claims you refuse to make.

## Founder expertise and worldview
- Strong opinions you genuinely hold.
- Stories, turning points, scars, case examples.
- Topics you can discuss for hours.
- Topics you avoid.
- Voice, tone, banned phrasing and phrases that sound naturally like you.

## Existing content and workflow
- Channels, output, formats and best/worst performers.
- Current team/contractors/tools.
- Founder hours per week.
- Where work stalls: ideas / scripts / recording / editing / approvals / publishing / learning.

## Sales and commercial feedback inputs
- Where call notes/transcripts live.
- Who will capture 'why they bought/didn't' after calls.
- CRM or manual pipeline source.
- What attribution is genuinely trackable today.

## Sign-off
- What Threadline owns.
- What the client owns.
- Approval SLA.
- Recording cadence.
- Day-7 dependencies.
