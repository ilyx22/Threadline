# Threadline Editor QA Checklist

## Before edit
- Correct script/version/source files.
- Correct platform/format/aspect ratio.
- Brand/voice requirements understood.
- Any factual on-screen text traced to approved script/source.

## Craft QA
- Hook lands immediately.
- Dead time removed without making delivery unnatural.
- Pacing supports comprehension, not ADHD editing for its own sake.
- Captions accurate and legible.
- Graphics reinforce meaning rather than decorate.
- Audio levels/noise acceptable.
- No copyrighted/uncleared assets.

## Commercial/brand QA
- No invented proof, statistics or claims.
- CTA matches approved strategy.
- Founder still sounds like the founder.
- No banned phrases/topics.
- Packaging supports the specific test/hypothesis.

## Handoff
- Correct filename/version.
- Revision notes attached.
- Export quality checked.
- Source/project files retained per policy.
