# Threadline Intelligence + Evidence Protocol

## Principle
No market finding becomes strategy because a model said it. Evidence -> observation -> candidate signal -> human decision -> test -> feedback.

## Evidence record
For every item store:
- Source URL or internal source reference.
- Source type.
- Published/observed date where available.
- Capture timestamp.
- Verbatim excerpt only where legally/operationally appropriate; otherwise a faithful summary.
- Who/what produced the source.
- Relevant customer/business segment.
- Confidence / limitations.

## Evidence classes
- Context: useful background, not directly decision-changing.
- Decision-relevant: directly informs the constraint/message/test.
- Suspect/faulty: plausible but unsupported, contradicted or low-trust.

## Candidate signal gate
A candidate signal must include:
- Claim/finding.
- Why it matters.
- Evidence references.
- Segment/maturity context.
- Confidence.
- Alternative explanation.
- Recommended content/commercial test.

If citations do not resolve, drop the candidate. Never attach a nearby source to make it look valid.

## Human decision
Approve / reject / edit. Machine confidence cannot auto-approve.

## Test design
- Hypothesis.
- Audience.
- Message/angle.
- Format/channel.
- Success metric.
- Read window.
- Expected upside / effort.

## Feedback
After distribution, write performance and commercial response back to the originating signal. Adjust confidence incrementally; do not rewrite history.
